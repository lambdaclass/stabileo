### BREAKING (results)

#### Saved concrete design inputs and curved wind pressure

With unified plates disabled, concrete projects retain their original default covers and explicitly selected stirrup and wall diameters when reopened or edited. Generated member wind loads use a compact fit when both halves follow the pressure profile and refine the whole band otherwise. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-plate-build-and-schedules.md).
