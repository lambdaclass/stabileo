### BREAKING (results)

#### Preserve shell loads at framed eaves and refuse ambiguous rafter offsets

Panel gravity assigns the roof load to a connected shell roof that fills its attached eave ring. It stops when a rigidly attached member would otherwise receive a second load by tributary width at an unresolved offset. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-gravity-eave-ring-and-offset-rafters.md). (#2512)
