### Fixed
#### Safety advisory for generated load combinations (2026-11-25)

[SA-2026-122](../docs/advisories/SA-2026-122-generated-load-combinations.md) covers incorrect shared alternatives, omitted manual roof live loads, lost flat-roof snow bases and obsolete wind cases after Auto Loads replacement. Verify the loads, rules and envelope membership, regenerate affected combinations and composite cases, and rerun analyses and design checks.
