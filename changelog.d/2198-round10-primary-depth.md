### BREAKING (results)

#### Verify primary shear with each actual cage diameter

Provided beam and wall primary shear now derives effective depth from the actual support, end or span cage, matching secondary shear. Replacing a nominal cage with a larger diameter can reduce capacity and change a prior pass to failure; smaller cages also receive their actual depth. Capacity, steel ratio and spacing use the same geometry. This corrects an error also present on main. Re-run affected designs and review primary shear.
