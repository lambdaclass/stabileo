### BREAKING (results)

#### Steel joints with a weak-axis moment are left unresolved (2026-11-03)

The joint typologies (shear tab, end plate, splice, gusset, base plate) read only each member's strong-axis end moment. A beam carrying a moment about its weak axis into a joint (pushed sideways, or under plan torsion) had it dropped, and the joint was designed and passed without it (#532). Such a joint is now left unresolved with the reason: release the moment if the joint is pinned about that axis, or design it by hand. A column running past the joint does not count.
