### Added
#### Homework and timed exams with an account (2026-11-10)

A signed-in teacher can assign one of their exercises as homework or as a timed exam, with a window and a duration. Students open the link, sign in and answer in a room without the solver or hints; answers save as they go and are handed in at the deadline, and results show when the teacher allows. The teacher sees each attempt and exports the grades as CSV. It is behind the `assignments_enabled` flag, off by default.
