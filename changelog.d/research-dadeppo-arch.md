### Added
#### DaDeppo–Schmidt deep arch benchmark

Research page at `/research/benchmarks/dadeppo-arch-1975`: one 215° clamped–hinged arch traced with the production corotational arc-length solver, shared native/WASM case, Stabileo viewer, precomputed replay and live recomputation. Stage: first version; round 2 reviewed by Kimi, Codex and Claude (2026-10-30). Compares the limit load (PR²/EI = 8.97) and the crown state at the limit point (u/R, v/R, rotation) with Table 1 of the 1975 paper as quoted by Schmidt (2000), with tolerances derived from a 20/40/80-element mesh study. Text in all six site languages.
