### BREAKING (results)

#### A compressed truss bar holds only its own steel check while the structure is stiff (2026-11-06)

The second-order screen of #1551 treated every compressed truss bar not held at both ends by supports as unbounded, so one tie rod in compression held every CIRSOC 301 check in the model. A truss bar is now judged on its own length against π²·E·I_min/L² and named by `LIN-SECOND-ORDER` or `LIN-SECOND-ORDER-NEAR`, holding only its own check, while the whole structure's elastic critical load factor α_cr under that result is at least 11, where its sway amplification 1/(1 − 1/α_cr) stays within 1.1. When α_cr is under 11, or the buckling solve that finds it refuses or fails, the frame stays `LIN-SECOND-ORDER-UNBOUNDED` and every check is held, as before. The buckling solve runs only when it can change the result. A combination keeps the bars bounded only while the sum of |factor|/α_cr over its cases stays within 1/11, and a combination records its own bars and α_cr bound, so a combination of combinations keeps them. Frame members are screened as before. The tie rods of the industrial building example are now tension-only, so that example's forces and displacements change; an example file's and a compact link's member behaviour (tension-only, compression-only, inactive) is now kept.
