### Changed
#### One title bar for the editor, and the panel on the left (2026-11-26)

On a desktop, Basic and PRO share one title bar. It holds the project button (|||), home, a File menu, save, undo, redo and a history timeline, followed by the project tabs. On the right are the panel-side switch, Report a bug, help, the language, the assistant, contact and settings. The Basic/PRO switch sits at the left of the ribbon. The right-hand panel opens on the left by default; the switch in the title bar moves it back and remembers the choice. The view controls float at the foot of the model: pointer, zoom to fit, home view and the view menu. Phones are unchanged.
