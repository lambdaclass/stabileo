### Added
#### PRO's videos beside the examples (2026-11-02)

The Project tab lists PRO's two videos beside the examples: how a building is modelled in PRO, and
the full walkthrough of a three-storey building from the grid to the report, each with what it
shows and how long it is, opened on YouTube in the interface's language where there is a version
in it. The first time PRO opens in a browser, and once a video is published, a card recommends the
short one; dismissed or followed, it does not come back. The videos take the place of a long guided
tutorial inside the app.
