### BREAKING (results)

#### Linear analysis warns when compression may amplify its moments past 10 % (2026-11-06)

A linear solve gave no warning when a member's compression was a large fraction of its critical load, for example a 3 m IPN 300 cantilever at 40 % of its Euler load, whose weak-axis moments are about 68 % larger in a second-order analysis (#1551). A linear static solve, 2D or 3D, now warns with `LIN-SECOND-ORDER` when N/Ncr may exceed 0.0748, where the worst elastic moment amplification, sec((π/2)·√(N/Ncr)), reaches 1.1, the limit CIRSOC 301 Appendix 7 takes for small second-order effects. The check uses member bounds only: a member clamped to a support at one end is judged against π²·E·I_min/(2L)², and one whose two ends sit on supports holding every translation against π²·E·I_min/L², each over its whole length and counting its axial span loads. A member over 0.0748/1.6, the largest CIRSOC 301 load factor, is named by `LIN-SECOND-ORDER-NEAR`. Any other compressed member warns `LIN-SECOND-ORDER-UNBOUNDED`, since its critical load cannot be bounded from the member alone. A load combination keeps the members its cases name and is unbounded when a case is near the limit or unbounded; combinations are not yet bounded on their own. Every CIRSOC 301 check is held, not passed, when any result warns `LIN-SECOND-ORDER` or `LIN-SECOND-ORDER-UNBOUNDED`, since H.1 takes the moments as second-order. Numerical values are unchanged.
