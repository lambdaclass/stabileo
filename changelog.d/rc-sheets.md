#### Reinforcement sheets: beams, columns, slabs, walls and punching, row by row (2026-11-08)

PRO gains "Reinforcement sheets" under Analyse, independent of the design module (which stays off by default), with the code they compute with shown on the panel, the sheets and the export (CIRSOC 201-2025 for now, stored with the project so other codes can be added). It reads the solved design combinations per physical member. Each physical beam is armed as one continuous run: bars top and bottom over its whole length, extra bars at the supports and in the spans cut where they are no longer needed and developed past it (9.7.3), the whole envelope checked station by station with the bars present there, then every span checked at both ends and at the section of its largest sagging moment, for sagging and hogging, each with its own combination; stirrup zones, splices, hooks, development lengths, an elevation and a bar schedule come with it; an extra bar is cut in a tension zone only where 9.7.3.5 allows it, and otherwise runs on to the support. Columns are proposed per stack, keeping their bar pattern up the floors and lapping it at each storey. Slabs, walls (meshed by the solve when drawn as one shell) and flat-slab punching are checked too. Options: moments at the support faces, the slab as a beam flange, and the special moment frame provisions of chapter 18 (capacity shear, confinement, strong column–weak beam, joint shear). Stabileo proposes the bars and iterates them until every check holds; the engineer adopts or edits them, and they are kept in the project. Each row lists every check with clause, demand, capacity and ratio, breaks the bars down by combination and opens into its step by step; the sheets export to Excel.
