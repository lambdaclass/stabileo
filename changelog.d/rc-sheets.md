#### Reinforcement sheets: main bars of beams, columns, slabs and walls row by row (2026-11-08)

PRO gains "Reinforcement sheets" under Analyse, independent of the design module (which stays off by default), with the code they compute with shown on the panel, the sheets and the export (CIRSOC 201-2025 for now, stored with the project so other codes can be added). It reads the solved design combinations per physical member: each beam span is checked at both ends and at the section of its largest sagging moment, for the largest sagging and hogging moment at each, each with its own combination and concurrent axial force; columns per storey; slab panels with Wood–Armer moments; walls per storey from the membrane forces at their base. Beams get flexure, minimum steel, fit, crack-control spacing, shear, torsion with its minimums and longitudinal bar rules, and skin steel past 90 cm; columns get slenderness and the moment magnifier, the steel ratio from the biaxial interaction surface, shear in both directions, ties and crossties; slabs get flexure, minimums, spacing and one-way shear; walls get in-plane axial and bending, in-plane shear, out-of-plane bending, minimum ratios, spacing and curtains. Stabileo proposes bars and iterates them until every check holds; the engineer adopts or edits them and they are kept in the project. Each row lists every check with its clause, demand, capacity and ratio, breaks the adopted bars down by combination, and opens into its step by step; the sheets export to Excel, one sheet per family. Development lengths, splices, seismic provisions, punching and drawings are out of scope.
