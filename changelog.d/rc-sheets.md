#### Reinforcement sheets: main bars of beams, columns and slabs row by row (2026-11-08)

PRO gains "Reinforcement sheets" under Analyse, independent of the design module (which stays off by default). It reads the solved design combinations per physical member: each beam is split into spans at its supports, each column into storeys, each slab panel is taken whole. Every row computes, with CIRSOC 201-2025 through the existing engine-backed checks, the bottom and top steel (span and supports), stirrups for shear and torsion and longitudinal torsion steel for beams; slenderness, the moment magnifier and the steel ratio from the biaxial interaction surface plus ties and crossties for columns; and Wood–Armer bottom and top steel in X and Y for slabs. Stabileo proposes bars, the engineer adopts or edits them, the adopted bars are checked at the depth they give and kept in the project, each row opens into its step by step, and the sheets export to Excel with one sheet per family. Development lengths, splices, seismic provisions and drawings are out of scope.
