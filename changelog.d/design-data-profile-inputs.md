### Fixed
#### Design inputs follow the active reinforcement model

The design-data panel shows the saved stirrup and wall bar diameters when the design uses those individual inputs. Sheet design options appear only when sheets are available. Both profiles keep the lateral-system and deflection-limit controls.

Links and restored tabs open the reinforcement sheet only in builds that offer it. Those builds also publish its report and command translations.

The published concrete editor assigns one family and material at a time. Its slab base inputs open directly. Bulk concrete assignment, the re-solve shortcut and sheet option descriptions follow their feature switch.

The published load dialog offers its implemented load-generation family. A saved foreign code keeps its identifier, settings and edition when reopened. The regulations panel and automatic load planner report that its module is unavailable in this profile.
