### Added
#### Arc-length snap-through research benchmark (2026-10-29)

Added a first version of the Riks–Crisfield engine-benchmark page that runs Stabileo’s production arc-length solver on shared two-bar cases, compares native and browser WASM results, traces deformed equilibrium paths, checks closed-form limit points, and opens the models in the editor.

The page reports the shallow-toggle reference failures explicitly and does not claim to reproduce any published example, table or figure. Editor sharing preserves the original MPa stiffness, and successful snap-through checks require a converged descending branch.
