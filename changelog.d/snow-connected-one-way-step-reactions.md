### BREAKING (results)

#### Transfer roof step drift across connected one-way bays

A constant roof step transfers drift to the bearings of every receiving rectangular one-way bay it covers. Regenerate snow loads on affected roofs. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-supported-deck-and-step-drift.md#transfer-roof-step-drift-across-connected-one-way-bays). (#2218)
