### BREAKING (results)

#### Keep automatic gravity on separate decks joined by ties

Panel-mode gravity checks separation across the overlap before assigning a bay, shell or member to a shared deck. Separate tied decks retain their loads, and one-way transfers on nearly level beams preserve the projected load resultant. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-gravity-physical-deck.md). (#2512)
