### BREAKING (results)

#### Block gravity when shells and beam bays claim the same deck area

Panel gravity generation refuses shells that overlap within the deck height tolerance or both claim the same part of a beam bay. It also refuses offset shells with vertically coupled eccentric connections to an overlapping loaded beam bay. See [the results changes](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-gravity-shared-deck-coverage.md). (#2284, #2299)
