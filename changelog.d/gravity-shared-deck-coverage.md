### BREAKING (results)

#### Block gravity when shells and beam bays claim the same deck area

Panel gravity generation refuses horizontal or sloped shells whose plan projections overlap within the deck height tolerance. It also refuses pairs that claim the same part of a beam bay. It refuses offset shells with vertically coupled eccentric connections to an overlapping loaded beam bay. See [the results changes](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-gravity-shared-deck-coverage.md). (#2284, #2299)
