### BREAKING (results)

#### Mesh CAD slabs along oblique wall traces

Supported CAD drafts share a continuous wall–slab mesh edge along oblique and interior-ending wall traces. Regenerate affected drafts before solving. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-cad-wall-slab-load-transfer.md#mesh-cad-slabs-along-oblique-wall-traces). (#2429)

#### Refuse automatic gravity on triangular slabs

Automatic gravity stops when triangular plates with plan area would be left out of the load path. Explicit surface pressures remain available. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-cad-wall-slab-load-transfer.md#refuse-automatic-gravity-on-triangular-slabs).
