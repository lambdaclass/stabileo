### BREAKING (results)

#### 3D: node rotations a connector couples but leaves free are refused (2026-11-08)

The shear springs of a finite-length 3D connector see e·(u_j − u_i) + L(e × e_x)·θ̄ with θ̄ the mean of the end rotations, so its rigid arms hold only that mean across the axis. When nothing else holds the rotations of its nodes (only trusses reach them, or the member there releases bending), the opposite rotations across the axis and the common twist about it cost no energy and the rotations are undetermined. The linear solver answered such models after a diagonal shift of 1.2e2 (sparse) or through LU (dense), with only a `StiffnessNotPositiveDefinite` warning, rotations the load did not determine, and translations off by up to 1.2e-9 relative (#1748). Every 3D analysis now factors the block of the assembled stiffness for the rotations the connectors couple, so partial member releases, rotational springs and supports count in each DOF exactly. When that block is singular, the model is refused with the connectors and nodes named. A rotation the orphan guard used to hold with artificial stiffness counts as free, so a free twist about a connector along a global axis is refused like the same twist about a skew one. Constraint nodes are not checked (#1749). Hold the rotations of one node with an unreleased member end, a shell or a rotational support, and give the connector the rotational springs (`kMoment` for twist, `kBendY`, `kBendZ`) the other node needs.
