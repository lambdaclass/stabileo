### BREAKING (results)

#### The default covers are CIRSOC 201-2025 Table 20.5.1.3.1's (2026-11-16)

A project that did not state its covers was designed with 25 mm everywhere, which leaves the main bars of a beam 33 mm deep behind Ø8 stirrups, under the 35 mm of Table 20.5.1.3.1. The defaults are now 30 mm for beams and columns, 25 mm for slabs and walls and 55 mm under footings, and the CIRSOC preset reads each family's own bars. In such projects beam and column capacities drop by about 1 to 2 %; projects that stored their covers give the same numbers. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-default-covers.md).
