### BREAKING (results)

#### 2D plastic collapse factor from a limit analysis, the same under any numbering, member direction and units (2026-11-05)

- Fix #758: the 2D plastic collapse factor came from where the step-by-step hinge history ended, and that history depends on the order in which ties between hinges are broken, so renumbering the nodes, reversing a member or changing the units moved it by up to 4.6× (engine `solve_plastic_2d`: a rigid-link beam 46.67 or 118.46, a fuzz frame 85.73 or 127.22). The factor is now the limit analysis's (static and kinematic theorems, by linear programming over the self-stress fields of the frame), which the engine and the app's 2D plastic collapse both report; the hinge history stays as the illustration of the mechanism, and when it ends away from the collapse load the result says so. A model that is a mechanism before any hinge forms is refused. The Basic course portal keeps λc = 71.296 (8(Mp,col + Mp,beam)/qL² = 1925/27); the engine's history had reported 71.529 for it. The limit analysis factor is reported as the collapse load only when something independent of its linear program confirms it: the exact plastic certificate (`certify::plastic`, rational arithmetic) brackets it, or, for models outside the certificate (constraints, inclined supports, axial span loads, loads on members of irrational length), the hinge history ends at the same load. Otherwise the result is not confirmed (`is_mechanism` false in the engine, `unresolved` in the app) and the factor is the lower of the two.
