### BREAKING (results)

#### Require CAD wall and beam alignment and resolve adjacent snow by eave span

CAD generation refuses a wall end that is within the CAD snap tolerance and off the beam trace. It also refuses uncertified beam traces in conforming slabs. Entered adjacent-roof snow surcharge applies to the eave spans outside the projected overlap with modeled geometry.
