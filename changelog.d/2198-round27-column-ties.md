### BREAKING (results)

#### Count anchored column tie legs and enforce minimum shear reinforcement

Recheck concrete columns with provided transverse reinforcement. An interior tie leg needs a longitudinal bar at each end. Unanchored legs can reduce shear or confinement capacity and invalidate a previously accepted cage. When shear reinforcement is required, a column with insufficient tie area fails even if its shear strength and tie spacing pass.
