#### Preserve certified CAD bearings and check adjacent roof snow drift

CAD drafts keep the beam edges shared with a conforming slab mesh. Snow fetch stops at an open joint, short clear spans retain their drift load, and an unsupported skewed transfer blocks snow generation.
