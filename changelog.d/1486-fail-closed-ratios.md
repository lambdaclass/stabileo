### BREAKING (results)

#### A design check that cannot be evaluated is refused, not passed with a ratio of 0 (2026-11-05)

A demand over a capacity that was zero, negative, NaN or infinite could come out as a ratio of 0, which reads as a pass: a CIRSOC 301 limit state whose slenderness or demand was NaN, a loaded bolt group without bolts or a weld without throat, a footing shear or punching check without a usable resistance, a non-positive allowable bearing pressure, or a CIRSOC 201 ductility ratio. Each such check is now listed as not evaluated (the member or footing is not marked OK), and any result holding NaN or infinity is withheld with a message naming the value. Models whose checks all had usable capacities give the same numbers. Every design, certify and postprocess ratio now goes through `engine/src/check.rs`, which returns a ratio or the reason it could not be formed, and `engine/src/boundary.rs` refuses any result with a non-finite number before it leaves the engine, naming its path (SA-2026-025, SA-2026-026, SA-2026-030, SA-2026-010, #1317, #1350).
