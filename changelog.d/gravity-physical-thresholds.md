### BREAKING (results)

#### Use physical clearance for automatic gravity

Automatic gravity clips framing by local headroom and evaluates pitched shells and bare beams at the exact member midpoint. If a noncoplanar bay straddles the cover threshold, automatic generation reports that it cannot determine the height.
