### Changed
#### The 3D view menu is shorter, and the sections are the physical geometry (2026-11-25)

The menu under the view cube groups the three axis views in one row and the projection in a
Perspective | Orthographic switch, and the clipping plane, the plan view and the distance measure
show a tick when they are on. In Basic and in PRO it draws the bars, and in PRO the plates, as lines
or with their sections; the thick lines stay in the settings, and in PRO «More view options» opens
the View panel at that choice.

The sections view and the physical geometry drew the same bars. They differed only in the slabs and
walls, which the physical geometry cut where the frame passes through them. They are now one view,
«Sections», which draws the slabs and walls cut. A saved project that showed the physical geometry
opens with the sections.

In the plan view a drag slides the floor with the pointer and stops when the pointer stops, without
the glide the 3D view keeps after a drag, and it slides over the level without rising or sinking.

The plan view looks straight down, where it used to lean a degree, and stays orthographic while it
is on. What lies wholly under the level is hidden; «Show below» shows it in halftone instead.
The cut is set as a height over the level, typed or stepped 5 cm with the arrows or the wheel. Two buttons turn the plan by 90°, and the axes in the corner turn with it.

The level is picked from the plan's own box by name and height, or, in a model without levels,
«Define levels» opens the step that creates them. A background drawing shows whole over the grid
of its level, and a cut on the level no longer breaks the grid and the drawing into flickering
dashes.
