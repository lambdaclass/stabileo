### Changed
#### The physical elements are read off the model as it changes, with nothing to recognise by hand (2026-11-10)

Columns, beams and braces were physical elements only after pressing "Recognise elements" in the Elements panel, the design desk's Prepare step or the reinforcement sheets, while slabs and walls had become plates on their own: a model was half read, and nothing said when the step was needed. Now in PRO the whole physical model is read as the model changes, keeping each element's id and name, in no undo step; what a person declared is never read again. A reading that would move a design decision stored for an element (a slab system, tendons, masonry, a phase) is not applied: the Elements panel and Diagnostics say how many wait, and the review opens from there. The "Recognise" buttons are gone. The plates panel counts plates and mesh elements apart, lists the mesh elements on demand, says a plate's state only when its mesh is missing, out of date or invalid, and moves the warning about corners connected to nothing to Diagnostics, by plate. The mesh dialog opens at 0.5 m for a plate made from a whole bay, and says the quality as good or how many elements are poor.
