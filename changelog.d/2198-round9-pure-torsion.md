### BREAKING (results)

#### Keep ordinary closed hoops eligible under pure torsion

Active torsion no longer imposes the secondary shear leg-distribution requirement when there is no checked secondary shear. The two outer legs remain available for torsion accounting, and nonzero secondary shear and torsion limits retain their checks. This corrects a false refusal on the pending #2198 branch. Seismic tests now verify both effective end cages, including an updated shared fallback. Five translated result notices restore omitted safety warnings, and German uses “Bügelschenkel.” See the [round-9 results record](../docs/results-changes/2198-round9-pure-torsion.md).
