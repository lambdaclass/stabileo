### Added
#### Basic's advanced functions solve and explain a structural analysis course, unit by unit (2026-10-30)

The force method lets you choose the primary structure, with axial stiffness on or off, bars and
springs written out term by term and the degree of indeterminacy explained. The classroom
displacement method counts and explains its kinematic unknowns. Influence lines cover N,
displacements and rotations at any section, member ends included, with envelopes from them and a
panel for load cases. Second order offers P-Delta with the geometric or the chord matrix and with
stability functions, at a chosen discretization, with its iterations step by step and a buckling
factor that converges. Plastic analysis shows each load step, the λ-displacement curve, plastic
rotations and the mechanism, checks a mechanism you place, and adds the static and kinematic
methods. Dynamics adds one- and many-degree-of-freedom systems with modal superposition, mass
models with condensation and point masses.
