### BREAKING (results)

#### Evaluate automatic gravity over overlapping deck footprints

Automatic gravity checks vertical ties, headroom and exposure over the overlapping footprints at their physical elevations. It asks for explicit loads when a nearby vertical tie makes ownership ambiguous or a single carrier would need different roof and floor actions. See [the gravity results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-gravity-local-deck-coverage.md). (#2543)
