#### Aluminium checks shear along the weak axis (2026-11-03)

The EN 1999-1-1 aluminium check carried the weak-axis shear of each member but had no row for it: a member under lateral shear (a rail or purlin under wind, a column swaying about its weak axis) passed with no shear check (#532). A `Shear, weak axis` row now checks it (6.2.6) with the shear area of the parts parallel to it: the flanges of an I, the walls along the width of a rectangular tube. A circular tube checks the resultant of its two shears.
