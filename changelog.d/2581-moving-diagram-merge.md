### BREAKING (results)

#### Keep support-face limits in exact moving-load envelopes

Moving-load shear and axial envelopes include the member-face limits from sampled
path joints and retain the extrema and jump faces of the exact member diagrams.
An axle directly over a restrained support appears in the reaction of that
position's solution. The one-sided member-face values contribute to the envelope.
See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-member-diagram-pieces.md).
