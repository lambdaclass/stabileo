### BREAKING (results)

#### Refuse modal mechanisms with translational inertia

Planar and spatial modal analysis now refuse when a node can translate with inertia and no resolvable restoring stiffness after constraints. Raw and certified analyses identify a detected mechanism by node and dominant displacement component. The stiffness check distinguishes a resolvable restoring force from cancellation. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-modal-massive-translation.md). (#2146, #2147, #2148, #2416, #2517)
