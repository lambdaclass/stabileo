#### Research: rate-dependent symmetry breaking in elastic snap-through, after Wang, …, Vella (PRL 2024) (2026-11-18)

A new research page, `/research/mechanics/vella-2024`, releases a steel strip clamped at ±α from its inverted shape and shows that a small asymmetry is amplified tens of times when the ends move slowly and not when they move fast. Statics use the production co-rotational element and arc-length; the snap uses an implicit nonlinear HHT-α integration written for this experiment. It reproduces the paper's shallow-theory bifurcation numbers, the natural frequency 57.86, the toy model's constants and the rate at which the amplification falls below 2; it does not reproduce the amplitudes of the paper's Fig. 2c (over 0.04 ≤ |µ̇| ≤ 0.1, the twelve-phase envelopes have median absolute relative errors of 73% for two clamps and 77% for one clamp) or the toy model's critical rate (+9 %), and the page counts those as failing comparisons. The research verification summary now marks a comparison with failing checks instead of showing a check mark. Research, not a design tool.
