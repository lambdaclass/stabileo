### Added
#### Published Lee-frame coverage for experimental 3D arc length (2026-11-25)

The experimental 3D arc-length solver now reproduces the shared Lee-frame
benchmark as a fully three-dimensional model. The test checks its first sampled
peak against the existing regression and against the published curve reading at
its documented one-percent precision, while requiring zero out-of-plane motion
and continuation through the descending branch. Solver numerics are unchanged.
