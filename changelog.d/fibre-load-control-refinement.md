### BREAKING (results)
#### Concrete fibre load control refines its stopping interval (2026-11-10)

Two-dimensional fibre runs with Hognestad concrete now cut back failed or non-positive-tangent trials from the last committed state. The stopping load interval is refined to the requested load-factor tolerance and returned as `loadLimit`, explicitly uncertified. The last converged equilibrium is not a capacity certificate. Re-run concrete fibre pushovers that stopped early; their carried load, displacements and reactions can change.
