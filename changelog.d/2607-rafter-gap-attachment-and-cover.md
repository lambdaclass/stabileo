### BREAKING (results)

#### Certify attached rafter gaps and their overhead roof cover

Panel gravity uses attached shell edges to determine rafter gap width and checks overhead roof strips loaded by tributary width. The planner blocks gaps whose shell attachment cannot be verified or whose mixed exposure requires separate pressures. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-rafter-gap-attachment-and-cover.md). (#2607)
