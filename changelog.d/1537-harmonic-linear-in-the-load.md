### BREAKING (results)

#### Harmonic analysis chooses between modal superposition and the direct solve per model and frequency, never per load (2026-11-06)

- Fix #1537, #1538, #1539, #1540 and #1541: since #1423 a frequency was solved directly when the residual of its modal response, which depends on the load, exceeded 10⁻², and the modal response was accepted below that. So a sum of loads could be solved one way and each load the other, which broke superposition and reciprocity, and accepted modal responses were off from the direct solve by up to 106 % at a node (a one-member 3D cantilever at 0.7 Hz: −9.14 instead of 139.4) with imaginary parts off by up to 8.6×. A 2D frame showed only the rotary-inertia warning. Now a model with fewer than 64 free DOFs is solved directly at every frequency (exact), and a larger one keeps the modal response only where the omitted modes are bounded (`q = |ω² − i a₀ω| / (λ_omit |1 + i a₁ω|)` at most 1/16, error under q² of their share) and solves the other frequencies directly, the same frequencies for every load. A modal response kept that way whose residual still exceeds 10⁻² is refused, never replaced for that load alone. Past the dense ceiling (more than 3000 DOFs) frequencies are not re-solved and HARM-TRUNCATION (#1434) still warns where the omitted modes are not corrected.
