### Added
#### Newmark (1959): one SDOF step response

First version (stage `first-version`, 0 reviews): a research page at
`/research/benchmarks/newmark-1959` runs the production WASM time-history solver
with Newmark average acceleration and compares an undamped axial SDOF step
response with its exact solution. Includes a shared JSON engine benchmark,
precomputed replay, Stabileo viewer, live recomputation, four time steps showing
period elongation, and English, Spanish and Portuguese text. Published figures,
damping, harmonic loads, nonlinear and multi-DOF examples are not yet covered.
