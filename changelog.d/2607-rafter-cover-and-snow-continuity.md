### BREAKING (results)

#### Account for stacked rafter gaps and connected snow roofs

A certified upper rafter gap can shelter the deck below it. Modeled step drift follows short continuous seams. Snow roof grouping includes joints along openings. Older projects with unequal rafter-gap end widths are flagged for load regeneration. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-rafter-cover-and-snow-continuity.md). (#2607)
