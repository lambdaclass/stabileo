### BREAKING (results)

#### Preserve automatic gravity on independent decks and lower roofs

Panel gravity keeps the loads of decks that share only one vertical restraint. Upper bays cover only their assigned regions, so a linked shell below a shadowed bay retains its roof live load. The planner refuses if a bay's generated transfer fails to carry its assigned area. See [the results changes](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-gravity-floor-coverage-and-transfer.md). (#2512)
