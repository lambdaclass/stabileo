### BREAKING (results)

#### Resolve longitudinal bars before checking CIRSOC 201 secondary shear

- Secondary shear and its interaction with torsion use the largest longitudinal bar in the layout to determine effective depth. Verification reports a refusal when the required bar geometry is unknown.
- The fixed and automatic column checks use the same longitudinal tension ratio as station verification for CIRSOC 201-2025 below the minimum transverse reinforcement. All secondary shear checks start at 0.001 kN. (#2171)
