### Changed
#### The analysis notice over the model folds to one line and can be closed (2026-11-08)

The notice that reports what the analysis could not verify (a known solver issue, the second order not bounded) used to cover the top of the model on a desktop with all its messages and no way to put it away. It now opens folded to its first message on every screen, with a button that counts the messages and opens the full list, and a close button. Closed, it stays away for the session until it has something different to say; its messages stay listed in PRO under Analyze › Diagnostics, with a button that puts it back, and in Basic in the results panel. A notice that explains why results are withheld cannot be closed.
