### BREAKING (results)

#### Recover drawing planes and preserve rotational constraint signs

Older planar projects recover the drawing plane from saved geometry and loads or request a plane selection before analysis. The constraint projection preserves signed rotational coefficients through reflected axes. Saved-result notices now distinguish the additional authored-load checks and accept legacy support rows identified by their table keys.
