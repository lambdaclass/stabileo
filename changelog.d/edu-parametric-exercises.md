### Added
#### Exercises that vary per student in Educational mode (2026-11-10)

A teacher can turn spans, heights and load magnitudes of an exercise into ranges, and let a support take other types. Each student enters their student number and gets a variant of their own, always the same one, and the teacher's review marks each hand-in against that student's structure. "Show variants" previews a few and checks that every one solves. The bundled exercises now vary too, and there are three new ones: a beam with an overhang, a Gerber beam and a portal whose bases change type.
