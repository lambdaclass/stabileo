### BREAKING (results)

#### Check CIRSOC 201 secondary shear against the secondary stirrup face

- Secondary shear and concurrent shear and torsion use the secondary face width, effective depth and stirrup legs. An unresolved cage or a nonrectangular section prevents verification of these checks. Active torsion requires a closed cage even when secondary shear is zero. (#2171)
- Provided reinforcement uses the largest spacing and fewest effective legs across the member for secondary shear and torsion. Missing coverage fails these checks under active demand.
