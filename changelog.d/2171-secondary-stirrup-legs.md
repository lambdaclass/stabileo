### BREAKING (results)

#### Verify secondary shear with the secondary stirrup legs

- CIRSOC 201 checks secondary shear and combined shear and torsion using two legs of a closed rectangular stirrup plus any declared secondary crossties. It fails checks that require more legs and refuses undetermined cages (#2171).
- Beam and wall verification uses the weakest stirrup spacing and leg counts across the member when the station cage is unresolved. Gaps fail under active demand. The combined check uses both effective depths from the actual stirrup diameter and the minimum steel for each active face.
