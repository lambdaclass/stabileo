### Fixed
#### Clarify moving-load shear limits in SA-2026-129 (2026-11-26)

[SA-2026-129](../docs/advisories/SA-2026-129-moving-load-force-jumps.md)
and its localized summaries distinguish the single combined shear jump
at exact coincidence from the one-sided shear limits between an axle and
a permanent point load. The workaround checks both limits.
