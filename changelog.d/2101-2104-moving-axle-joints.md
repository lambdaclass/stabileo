### BREAKING (results)

#### Resolve moving axles at path joints before recovering shear

Moving-load sweeps now classify joint axles in cumulative path coordinates and include the member-face limits where a sampled axle lands on a joint. An exact sample with the axle directly over a restrained node carries that load in the support reaction. The one-sided entries contribute only to the moving envelope. For one axle on a simply supported span, the shear magnitude reaches the axle weight at a support face when the grid samples that support. The browser spatial envelope recovers axial force on both sides of interior axles and at sampled joint faces. Planar truss faces retain the signed axial component of the axle load. Joint resolution accounts for path coordinate precision and each axle’s sampled arithmetic. The inclined-support and tip-spring envelopes in #2101 and #2104 are invariant under rotation and reflection. Indexed train positions retain axles at the path entrance and reversed members preserve coincident interior loads. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-moving-axle-joints.md).
