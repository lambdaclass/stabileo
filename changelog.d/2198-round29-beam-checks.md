### BREAKING (results)

#### Align beam checks and detailing with the verified reinforcement

Recheck beam secondary shear, combined shear and torsion at hogging stations, and detailing with regional stirrup overrides. Secondary shear reports the concrete strength of the selected cage and credits developed lateral corner bars where their positions are established. Shear and torsion read the same primary tension depth. Detailing shares one seating diameter, and the seismic retrofit preserves the existing cages when the checked pitch cannot fit.
