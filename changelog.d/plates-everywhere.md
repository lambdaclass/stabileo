### Changed
#### Every slab and wall is a plate you select whole and mesh after making it (2026-11-08)

Plates drawn by their outline could be selected whole and meshed from their panel after they were made, but the rest of the model's shells could not: an example, an older project, an IFC import, elements drawn corner by corner, and the slabs and walls of the building tool, which were meshed by a separate "mesh at solve" path. Now there is one way. Shells that are not plates are listed in the Plates panel, which makes them plates in one click (all of them, or the ones selected; the quick editor does it for one): each panel of shells becomes a plate whose outline and holes are its boundary, its shells kept as its current mesh so the analysis answer does not change until it is meshed again, and a uniform area load on every shell of a slab becomes the plate's own load, which follows any mesh. The building tool places its slabs and walls as plates, meshed now or by the analysis; a slab plate's mesh keeps the columns' sections in it (lines on their faces, the nodes inside tied to the column), a waffle slab is meshed no coarser than its ribs, and mesh nodes on an outline side whose two corners carry the same plain support get it too. Projects saved with slabs waiting for the solve open them as plates. Meshing several plates together leaves every one current. The old mesh generator is gone from the Plates panel; a circular plate, with a circular hole if wanted, is made from its own form and meshed from its panel like any other, and curved surfaces have their own section. A floor load can be put on a slab plate before it has a mesh.
