### BREAKING (results)

#### Verify shear against the longitudinal cage and carry accepted ties into drawings

Recheck rectangular concrete columns and beams with multilayer support reinforcement. The column checks use the resolved bar centres and area in each direction. Beam support shear uses the tension face of the concurrent bending demand. Drawings use the verified rectangular column tie diameter and respect its spacing. A beam line with an uncovered or unrepresentable stirrup region requires review. [Results and verification](../docs/results-changes/2198-round28-shear-geometry.md)
