### BREAKING (results)

#### Gravity on an independently supported bay below a pitched shell

Panel gravity keeps both loads when independent supports establish a separate bay below a pitched shell. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-supported-deck-and-step-drift.md#gravity-on-an-independently-supported-bay-below-a-pitched-shell). (#2291)

#### Exact step drift on a one-way roof

A rectangular one-way receiving bay with a constant step profile and complete bearings now receives the exact pressure-triangle reactions. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-supported-deck-and-step-drift.md#exact-step-drift-on-a-one-way-roof). (#2218)
