#### Free-body diagrams as a step of Educational exercises (2026-11-11)

An exercise whose structure has two or more bars, such as a portal frame or a Gerber beam, now asks for the free-body diagram of each bar between the reactions and the diagrams. Elements in a straight line with no hinge between them count as one bar, so a beam with loads and supports along it asks nothing. For each end the student enters what the joint exerts on the bar, with the reactions' signs, and a drawing of the cut bar shows each force and moment as it is typed, beside the bar's own loads. Checking says, for a wrong bar, whether the student's diagram is in equilibrium at all. Exam marking and hand-ins include these answers, and an author can turn the step off.
