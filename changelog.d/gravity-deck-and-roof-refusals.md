### BREAKING (results)

#### Refuse ambiguous automatic gravity and roof loads

Load generation blocks when one shell would absorb overlapping bays on separate decks or when an overlapping shell has ambiguous unequal-offset attachments to its bay. Members whose midpoint lies exactly on a collinear roof edge are classified as covered. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-gravity-deck-and-roof-refusals.md). (#2512)
