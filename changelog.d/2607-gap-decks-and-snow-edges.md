### BREAKING (results)

#### Classify attached roof gaps and snow drift in their physical geometry

Panel gravity refuses gaps on separate roof decks attached to one rafter and checks gap exposure at the roof plane. Snow drift recognizes near-coincident edges and retains the surcharge across a gap when an intervening roof lies below the receiver.
