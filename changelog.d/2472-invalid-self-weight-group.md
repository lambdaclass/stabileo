### BREAKING (results)

#### Refuse invalid self-weight groups

Analysis refuses self-weight rules with missing groups or malformed membership, and earlier saved results are flagged for recheck. Explicitly empty groups remain valid. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-invalid-solver-numbers.md#refuse-self-weight-with-a-missing-group-or-invalid-membership). (#2472)
