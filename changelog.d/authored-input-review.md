### BREAKING (results)

#### Catch mistyped analysis settings and constraints

Analysis, snapshot restore and fixture apply identify unknown fields before a gravity rule or a constraint can take an unintended default. A planar eccentric connection can be solved when its in-plane releases remove the coupling to out-of-plane motion. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-malformed-structural-input.md).

#### Show saved-model notices for malformed numeric loads

The recalculation notice also identifies incomplete or nonfinite load inputs that analysis refuses before projection. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-malformed-authored-loads.md).
