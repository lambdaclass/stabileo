### BREAKING (results)

#### Effective stirrup legs are specific to each shear direction

Fix CIRSOC 201 secondary shear and combined shear–torsion verification to use the effective stirrup legs in each direction. Older closed rectangular cages default to two secondary legs; additional horizontal crossties can be entered separately. Torsion continues to credit only one leg of the outer closed hoop. Both directions now fail independently of strength when the provided legs fall below the transverse-spacing requirement, without increasing the reinforcement. Recheck saved concrete member designs; an overall pass can become a failure. See the [results-change record](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-directional-stirrup-legs.md) (#2171).
