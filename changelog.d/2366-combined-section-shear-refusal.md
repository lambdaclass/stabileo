### BREAKING (results)
#### Refusal for combined torsional and transverse shear

Detailed section stress now withholds combined shear, derived stresses and pass/fail when torsional and transverse shear are both nonzero at the evaluated fibre or cut sample. Individual stress components remain available. See [the results change](../docs/results-changes/2026-11-combined-section-shear-refusal.md) (#2366).
