### BREAKING (results)

#### Linear analysis warns when a member turns past 0.1 rad inside its span (2026-11-06)

`LIN-SMALL-DISPLACEMENT` judged node rotations and each member's end chord only, so a member fixed at both ends under a large span load, which moves nothing at its ends, was never warned though it turned past 0.1 rad inside the span (#1506). A linear static solve, 2D or 3D, now recovers each frame member's section rotation along its span from the same exact Euler–Bernoulli / Timoshenko deflection the deformed shape draws (end displacements and rotations in the member's axes, releases, distributed and point span loads, and 2D span couples), on each interval between 64 stations and the load positions, where each plane's moment is a cubic whose zeros, the rotation's extremes, are found exactly, and warns where it exceeds 0.1 rad; in 3D the two bending planes and the twist are judged together, the norm of the rotation vector bounded on each interval by the norm of each part's largest magnitude there. With every section rotation within 0.1 rad, an Euler–Bernoulli member's deflection from its chord stays within L/20. Each result records the member data the check needs (`resultSummary.memberSpans`), and a load combination recomputes the rotations from its own displacements and span loads; when a contributing case lacks the record, the combination carries the member warnings of its cases as before. Not covered yet, tracked in #1569: the slope a Timoshenko member adds by shear deformation, and curvature from a thermal gradient in a member with a release. Numerical values are unchanged.
