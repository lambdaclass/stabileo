### Fixed
#### Load combinations of a model with shells report their shell stresses (2026-11-07)

`solve_multi_case_3d` and `combine_results_3d` returned every combination without plate stresses, quad stresses or quad nodal stresses, and said nothing about it (#1673). They now sum each component (σxx, σyy, τxy, mx, my, mxy, qx, qy, and the nodal stresses) with the combination's factors and recompute the von Mises and principal stresses from the sums, never by combining them. A plate's face stresses need its thickness: `combine_results_3d` takes `plateThicknesses` and refuses a plate without one. A combination is refused when its cases list their shells differently, or when two shells of different families share an id (#1666). The nodal von Mises lists are not recomputed, since the cases do not carry what they are made from; they are left out of a combination, as before. The envelope's maximum-magnitude result still carries no shell stresses; the app takes them from the shell combination kernel.
