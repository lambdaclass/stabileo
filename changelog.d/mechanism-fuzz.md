#### Mechanism fuzzing of the linear certificate (2026-11-05)

A seeded generator builds 2D and 3D models that are mechanisms or near-mechanisms by construction (all-hinged or all-released nodes, connectors without moment stiffness, missing or partial supports, concurrent or parallel inclined supports, rigid links and diaphragms that carry a free rotation, a skew rotation left by a torsion release, and tiny rotational springs with a closed-form answer), each with its zero-energy modes known. The certified linear solve must refuse them, leave them uncertified, or be right: a certified result whose loads do work on a zero-energy mode, that moves along one, or that misses the closed-form answer fails. `make test.engine.mechanism-fuzz.quick` runs a checked-in seed corpus (for the per-PR tier) and `make test.engine.mechanism-fuzz.long DURATION=30m` runs fresh seeds and writes minimised reproducers (for the nightly and weekly tiers); the testing-cadence framework schedules them. Open issues they still hit (#458, and #1437 which it found: a soft rotational spring at a hinged 2D node replaced by the artificial stiffness) are listed seeds and regression files that fail once the issue is fixed; #1414, fixed by #1415, keeps a regression file that must be refused.
