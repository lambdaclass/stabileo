# Stabileo 0.4.0: the design module, off by default while it is finished, and four safety fixes

**English** · [Español](v0.4.0.es.md) · [Português](v0.4.0.pt.md) · [Deutsch](v0.4.0.de.md) · [Français](v0.4.0.fr.md) · [Türkçe](v0.4.0.tr.md)

Released 2026-10-28. The full technical record is in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md); the
advisories are in [`docs/advisories/`](../advisories/README.md).

> **Note on timing.** stabileo.com deploys every change merged into `main`, so the fixes below
> went live between 21 and 26 October 2026, as each was merged. The app shows, once, a notice that
> results may have changed, and projects saved before a change that concerns them show a re-check
> prompt when they are opened.

> **Normative design is now off by default in PRO.** Sizing, code checks (CIRSOC 201, 301 and the
> others), reinforcement, detailing and drawings are in development. PRO starts with modelling,
> loads and combinations, the analysis and its results, the calculation exports and IFC; none of
> them needs design. To use design, turn on **Settings → PRO → "Turn on normative design (in
> development)"**, which lists what it does not cover yet. The choice is remembered per browser. A
> project that already holds design (bars, detailing, decisions) keeps all of it with design off,
> and offers to turn it on. While design is off, nothing of it runs: no sizing, no code check, no
> verification on a re-run.

After 0.3.0 we turned the verification from the solver to what surrounds it: the loads the app
generates, the design checks, and the path from a solve to a design. That found four more defects
that could give a wrong or unconservative result, one of them critical. They are fixed, and each
one has a published safety advisory saying what was wrong, who is affected and what to re-check.

---

## 1. Your results may change: re-run your projects

Re-run the projects that use the features below and compare with the results you already used.
Each row links to its advisory, with a worked case and what to do with results you delivered.

| Advisory | What was wrong | Severity | Who is affected, and what to re-check |
|---|---|---|---|
| [SA-2026-021](../advisories/SA-2026-021-free-roof-wind-minimum.md) | Free roofs (CIRSOC 102): no wind at all along the ridge, and across it less than the §2.1.5 minimum of 0,75 kN/m² × Af (17 % of it at 20 m/s) | High | Automatic wind loads with the free-roof structure (sheds, canopies, car-park roofs). Re-check the longitudinal bracing, its connections and the columns' weak-axis bending |
| [SA-2026-022](../advisories/SA-2026-022-earth-pressure-dead-load-resisting.md) | With earth pressure H, the CIRSOC 101 combinations where the dead load resists it (0,9 D + 1,6 H, 1,2 D + 1,6 H) were never generated (27 to 45 % low in an example) | High | Models with H loads (soil pressure on walls, or an H load case). Re-check overturning, sliding and reverse moments of retaining and basement walls |
| [SA-2026-023](../advisories/SA-2026-023-column-moment-sign.md) | The CIRSOC 201 column check read one sign of the moment: a layout with more bars on one face passed on its stronger side (φMn up to 38 % high) | Critical | RC columns in uniaxial bending with 6, 10, 14, 18 or 22 bars from the designer, or unequal bars on opposite faces. Re-check every such column that passed; re-running the design may add bars |
| [SA-2026-024](../advisories/SA-2026-024-design-on-unfactored-results.md) | Footings, joints, slabs, walls and the steel optimiser designed for the unfactored loads when combinations were defined but not solved (live calc, a failed combination solve, or an empty active list): 15 % low in an example, 29 to 38 % for D + L | High | PRO 3D models with combinations whose design was read with live calc on, after a combination solve failed, or with no active combination. Re-solve and redesign; a footing that lists "Active result set (no combination solved)" in a model with combinations was designed this way |

**Other changes that move results** (not defects with an advisory; each is in the re-check
prompt, and described in [`docs/results-changes/2026-10-design-chain.md`](../results-changes/2026-10-design-chain.md)):

| Change | Who is affected, and what to re-check |
|---|---|
| Modal, response-spectrum and time-history analyses, and the modal step of the seismic and wind load plans, refuse member insertion offsets and shell offsets: they were solved on the node lines, without the eccentricities the static analysis takes (#460) | Models with offsets that relied on modal or spectral results. Use static seismic forces, or remove the offsets, until the dynamic chain takes them |
| CIRSOC 301 steel verdicts (#461): a limit state the engine cannot evaluate holds the member («not evaluated») instead of failing it; undeclared fabrication (rolled or welded) and load height are checked both ways; H.1 is rated on forces that act together, so some ratios fall; unbraced lengths per axis and per flange; weak-axis shear (G.6) is checked; 0.95 to 1.00 reads as a warning | Steel members checked with design on. Cold-formed sections are no longer checked by CIRSOC 301 as if hot-rolled: CIRSOC 303 is not implemented, so they read not designed, and an earlier pass is not a valid verification |
| Aluminium, timber and composite members are checked by their own checker, no longer as CIRSOC 301 steel (#463) | Members of those materials checked with design on |
| Concrete beams (#462, #475): a beam that does not declare its lateral role is designed as part of the lateral system; minimum shear reinforcement and torsion are checked; an upright beam under a slab is designed about its depth instead of refused as biaxial | Concrete beams checked with design on |
| Strip footings under walls are checked along the whole strip, not under each foot of the wall on its own (#475) | Wall footings designed with design on |

**Also changed:** the site altitude for wind now accepts values below sea level, where Ke is
above 1 (#581).

## 2. Warned or refused instead of silently wrong

- **No design over an unsolved set of combinations.** With combinations to design for and none
  of them solved (or only SRSS or ABS ones active, or an empty active list), every design refuses
  and says why. "Re-run analysis and design" fails, and the project stays marked as needing a
  re-run, when the combinations were refused or failed.
- **What the design cannot evaluate reads «not evaluated» or «held»,** with its reason, and is
  never counted as passing.
- **Dynamic analyses refuse member and shell offsets** instead of solving without them (above).
- **A load on a member or node that does not exist is refused,** naming the load and the number,
  by the solve and by renumbering. Before, a member load like that was skipped without a word, and
  renumbering could move a deleted support's settlement onto another support.
- **A cable whose own weight gives more than a quarter of its tension is warned** when it has no
  pretension: a heavy member typed as a cable is usually a truss member.
- **The re-check prompt covers everything above,** and now also names the 0.3.0 fixes to projects
  solved on a build between their merge and the release, which it had missed.

## 3. Other fixes

- **The combination path checks for NaN displacements** before it publishes results, as the single
  solve does, and every manual solve cancels a pending live calc, which could report "the model
  changed" for an untouched model.
- **One manual solve.** Enter, the ribbon, the exports and the steel optimiser's re-verify go
  through the same solve as Calcular, with its checks, and a solve started before a change to the
  self-weight or the solve settings no longer publishes its answer under the new settings.
- **Bar marks count every stirrup leg.** The PRO verification report scheduled a 4-leg stirrup as
  one 2-leg hoop, so its cut list and steel weight left out the inner legs; they are now listed as
  crossties, and the table is translated (it printed internal keys). Quantities only.
- **The default steel declares its ultimate strength.** "Acero A36", the material every new model
  starts with, had no Fu, so every CIRSOC 301 check of a new model read "not evaluated" until the
  user typed it. It now has Fu = 400 MPa.
- **Deleting or renumbering no longer leaves a load on a number someone else takes:** a deleted
  floor-load definition takes its loads with it, deleting a member takes its tendon and deleting a
  node its imposed displacement, and a spectrum's number is never given again (a spectral case on
  a deleted spectrum solved on the new one's table).
- **Editing a spectrum retires the results solved on the old one,** and undo and redo do the same.
  Before, D + Ex solved on a 0.4 g spectrum stayed on screen after the spectrum became 0.8 g.
- **The PRO DXF of an envelope draws the envelope.** It drew one load state built from each end's
  largest force (320.59 kN·m on a beam whose envelope peaks at 49.16); envelope and SRSS sets no
  longer write a deformed shape.

## 4. New

- **The design chain, behind the switch above:** concrete design and detailing, other materials
  and codes, documents and the design workspace. It is in development. Its comparison with the
  original design of a rebuilt six-storey building is an internal regression check, not an
  independent validation.
- **A server backend with user accounts** is in the repository. It is off in production: the app
  works as before, with no account.
- **PRO modelling, on the way to whole buildings:** plates as physical objects meshed on demand,
  with Diagnostics on the Analyse row; curved members as whole pieces with loads along the arc and
  plates with curved sides; DXF templates over several levels, CAD-style snaps and per-floor plans
  in the CAD wizard; use zones per floor with their live loads from the CIRSOC 101 table, and
  finishes. A member cut by a mesh keeps its loads on its pieces, and the IFC import keeps every
  straight piece of a curved edge on its own points.
- **Cable pretension.** Specifications › Members › Cable takes a pretension (kN), from which the
  engine sets the cable's unstressed length. This answers the 0.3.0 limitation on heavy cables
  (#527); without a pretension the drawn chord is the unstressed length, as before.

## 5. Faster and smaller

- **The landing page and the blog load 0.46 MB instead of 6.4 MB:** they no longer download the
  whole application and engine. The app loads them on its first screen, as before.
- **The engine is 25 % smaller** (12.0 MB to 9.0 MB), now optimized with `wasm-opt`. CI checks on
  every run that the optimized engine gives the same results, bit for bit, as the unoptimized one
  on the 184 determinism cases.
- **For contributors:** the engine's tests build in 7 binaries instead of 104 (rebuilds three
  times faster), `make test-changed` and a pre-push hook run the tests a change most likely
  affects, and the end-to-end suite runs in ten balanced shards with a quarantine for flaky tests.

## 6. Known limitations

- As in [0.3.0](v0.3.0.md#4-known-limitations), except heavy cables, which now take a pretension.
- Normative design is in development; what it does not cover yet is listed under its switch:
  cold-formed steel (CIRSOC 303) is not designed, E.4 of singly symmetric steel sections is not
  evaluated, EC3 lateral-torsional buckling (6.3.3) is evaluated in part.
- Dynamic analyses refuse models with member or shell offsets (IFC calculation models have them).
