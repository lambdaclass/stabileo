Released 2026-11-18. These notes cover changes since **v0.6.1**. The full record is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Your results may change: re-run affected projects

Re-run affected concrete and steel checks and member-load analyses. For affected generated snow, roof and floor area loads, **regenerate the loads first**, then re-run load cases, combinations, envelopes and derived design checks. Re-solving stored loads alone does not repair them. Compare deflections, demands, reinforcement, utilization, governing combinations and certificates with results already used. Review delivered calculations and sizing. A refusal, unavailable quantity or unchecked reinforcement is no passing answer and is not zero demand.

Regenerate affected wind loads and width-mode gravity and seismic actions. Regenerate snow combinations where the companion snow basis was affected. Review ambiguous or unknown 3D supports against their intended restraints before rerunning analysis.

## Other changes

Hungarian release guidance and public copy are available. Mohr stress calculations and shared links preserve small nonzero stresses; support-reaction teaching explanations are corrected. Reports and downloads refuse results changed during sign-in. Optional research, tools and diagram-game additions remain disabled by default; existing public pages remain available.

Automatically generated loads and reinforced-concrete design and detailing results now carry an audit notice with links to open findings. Nonlinear analysis stays responsive during a solve and supports progress and cancellation. Large 3D combinations and repeated design checks reuse more work. The landing page rotates through live structural figures. The optional research collection adds a masonry-arch experiment based on Heyman’s *The Stone Skeleton*.

## Known limitations

**Known wrong results remain open.** An internal audit of load generation and concrete design is in progress, with open findings. This release does not fix the issues below. Read their affected scope and independently verify affected results before use.

- Generated gravity, wind, snow and seismic loads: [#2202](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2202), [#2208](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2208), [#2209](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2209), [#2218](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2218).
- Concrete reinforcement and member checks: [#2212](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2212), [#2213](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2213), [#2214](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2214), [#2215](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2215), [#2216](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2216), [#2217](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2217), [#2225](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2225).
- Member-load recovery and restrained-support reactions: [#2226](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2226), [#2227](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2227).
- Ground-motion symmetry: [#2125](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2125).

- **[SA-2026-056](../advisories/SA-2026-056-constrained-shell-dynamic-invariance.md) remains unfixed:** independently verify dynamic demands for constrained mixed frame-and-shell models that change materially after renumbering, rotation or unit conversion. No conservative correction factor is established.
- 2D plastic collapse remains refused pending [#758](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/758), as described in [SA-2026-044](../advisories/SA-2026-044-plastic-2d-invariance.md). A certified 3D lower bound does not certify the raw hinge history.
- Time history does not provide total dynamic support demands. Use an independent analysis including inertia, damping and support loading. Unsupported cable analyses and nonzero prescribed support motion in cable/time-history workflows refuse explicitly. Spatial arc-length and displacement-control solvers are not added.
- Snow loading still carries a disclosed one-sided torsion assumption ([#2224](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2224)). Independently account for the distributed torque from eccentric tributary loads.
