# Stabileo 0.6.2: corrected member recovery, design checks and generated loads

**English** · [Español](v0.6.2.es.md) · [Português](v0.6.2.pt.md) · [Deutsch](v0.6.2.de.md) · [Français](v0.6.2.fr.md) · [Magyar](v0.6.2.hu.md) · [Türkçe](v0.6.2.tr.md)

Released 2026-11-16. These notes cover changes since **v0.6.1**. The full record is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Your results may change: re-run affected projects

Re-run affected concrete and steel checks and member-load analyses. For affected generated snow, roof and floor area loads, **regenerate the loads first**, then re-run load cases, combinations, envelopes and derived design checks. Re-solving stored loads alone does not repair them. Compare deflections, demands, reinforcement, utilization, governing combinations and certificates with results already used. Review delivered calculations and sizing. A refusal, unavailable quantity or unchecked reinforcement is no passing answer and is not zero demand.

## Safety advisories

The following six High-severity advisories are fixed in **0.6.2**. Follow each advisory for its affected inputs and user action, even if earlier results passed or were certified.

| Advisory | Severity | What changed and what to do |
|---|---|---|
| [SA-2026-069](../advisories/SA-2026-069-torsion-secondary-shear.md) | High | The torsion section limit omitted concurrent secondary shear (#2142). Re-run concrete designs and review section failures and missing stirrups; adding stirrups cannot repair an exceeded section limit. |
| [SA-2026-070](../advisories/SA-2026-070-interior-member-couples.md) | High | Interior member deflection and rotation omitted span loads (#2143). Review deformed shapes, education readouts and steel/concrete serviceability checks, including full and partial trapezoids. Loaded recovery without sufficient EI now refuses explicitly. |
| [SA-2026-071](../advisories/SA-2026-071-incomplete-concurrent-demands.md) | High | Member verification could pass with incomplete concurrent demands (#2096). Re-run concrete and steel designs and review combinations, members, stations and certificates. |
| [SA-2026-072](../advisories/SA-2026-072-snow-ridge-subdivision.md) | High | Unequal roof-edge subdivision could move the snow ridge and change generated loads (#2144). Regenerate snow loads, then rerun analyses, combinations and design checks. Re-solving stored loads alone does not correct them. |
| [SA-2026-073](../advisories/SA-2026-073-snow-crossing-moment.md) | High | Members crossing a snow ridge received one uniform pressure and lost the physical load moment. Loads now split at the physical ridge. Regenerate all affected generated area loads, then rerun analyses, combinations and design checks. Re-solving stored generated loads alone does not correct them. (#2176). |
| [SA-2026-074](../advisories/SA-2026-074-snow-panel-subdivision.md) | High | Subdividing a sloped rafter could classify an explicit roof panel as a floor and silently remove its eave snow. Panel roof classification now uses physical panel footprints. Regenerate all affected generated area loads, then rerun analyses, combinations and design checks. Re-solving stored loads alone does not correct them. (#2172). An unsplit sloped rafter over a separate lower panel could also misclassify that panel as a roof, changing roof/floor area loads and silently adding snow in width mode. |

## Other changes

Hungarian release guidance and public copy are available. Mohr stress calculations and shared links preserve small nonzero stresses; support-reaction teaching explanations are corrected. ## Known limitations

**Known wrong results remain open.** An internal audit of load generation and concrete design is in progress, with open findings. This release does not fix the issues below. Read their affected scope and independently verify affected results before use.

- **[SA-2026-056](../advisories/SA-2026-056-constrained-shell-dynamic-invariance.md) remains unfixed:** independently verify dynamic demands for constrained mixed frame-and-shell models that change materially after renumbering, rotation or unit conversion. No conservative correction factor is established.
- 2D plastic collapse remains refused pending [#758](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/758), as described in [SA-2026-044](../advisories/SA-2026-044-plastic-2d-invariance.md). A certified 3D lower bound does not certify the raw hinge history.
- Time history does not provide total dynamic support demands. Use an independent analysis including inertia, damping and support loading. Unsupported cable analyses and nonzero prescribed support motion in cable/time-history workflows refuse explicitly. Spatial arc-length and displacement-control solvers are not added.
- Snow loading still carries a disclosed one-sided torsion assumption ([#2224](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2224)). Independently account for the distributed torque from eccentric tributary loads.
