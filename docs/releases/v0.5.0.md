# Stabileo 0.5.0: wrong results fixed in design, the solver and dynamics, PRO's analysis mode, and every open defect contained

**English** · [Español](v0.5.0.es.md) · [Português](v0.5.0.pt.md) · [Deutsch](v0.5.0.de.md) · [Français](v0.5.0.fr.md) · [Türkçe](v0.5.0.tr.md)

Released 2026-11-DD. The full technical record is in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md); the
advisories are in [`docs/advisories/`](../advisories/README.md).

> **Note on timing.** stabileo.com deploys every change merged into `main`, so the fixes below
> went live between 29 October and 6 November 2026, as each was merged. Every change that can move
> a result has its own results version (6 to 42 in this release). A project solved before a change
> that concerns it shows a re-check notice when it is opened, now in all six of the app's languages.

After 0.4.0 we kept looking for wrong results: in the design checks, the load generator, the
solver's nonlinear and buckling paths, and the 3D-to-2D cut. That found nineteen defects with a
published safety advisory, one of them critical (SA-2026-029, the CIRSOC 201 column design).
They are fixed. Every wrong-result issue still open is now warned about or refused by name until its
fix lands ([#807](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/807)).

## 2. Warned or refused instead of silently wrong

- **Every open wrong-result issue is contained** ([#807](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/807)): the analysis it concerns warns, or refuses by name,
  until the issue's fix lands. The list, with each warning's code, is in
  [`docs/verification/CONTAINMENT.md`](../verification/CONTAINMENT.md).
- **A modal analysis of a mechanism, a non-finite time history and an envelope that overflows are
  refused** instead of shown.
- **A 3D model that a 2D cut cannot represent is refused** (skew section axes, a section that would
  need flipping, a constraint that ties in-plane to out-of-plane movement).
- **ACI 318 and EN 1992 members with torsion read "torsion not checked"** instead of passing.
- **A share link with non-finite numbers or references to missing nodes is refused,** and a file
  saved by a newer Stabileo is refused with a message that says so.
- **"Check an exported report…" fails closed:** a report whose case and numbers were swapped, or
  with sections removed, no longer checks as reproduced. It confirms a report agrees with its own
  record; it does not prove who made it.

## 3. Other fixes

- **The equilibrium check names the loads it could not add up,** and a balanced case is listed even
  when another is not.
- **PRO design no longer counts every member as steel** when a concrete material has no f′c.
- **The 3D industrial shed example** opens with its own CIRSOC 101-2025 combinations; its old
  "1.2D + L + E" label was the numbers of 1.2 D + 1.0 Lr.
- **The influence line of the Tools** gave a shear 200 % off just past a support.
- **Load combinations add their cases in the same order on every solve,** so a combined force no
  longer moves in its last digit between builds.
- **The calculation report prints the result set you choose,** by default the envelope of the
  combinations, instead of the sum of every case at factor 1.

## 4. New

- **PRO's analysis mode is complete:** help lines under each option, view categories and named
  views, a linked IFC as a 3D reference, command search, a split 3D and plan view, master and
  similar storeys, plate results (membrane forces, transverse shears, principal moments, faces), a
  report after the analysis, and a read-only link to a model.
- **Basic's structural analysis course**, units 1 to 7: the force method, the classroom
  displacement method, influence lines, P-Δ and buckling, plastic analysis step by step, and
  dynamics, each explained step by step.
- **German, French and Turkish,** for the app, the site and the re-check notices.
- **Stabileo Research and Tools:** 17 runnable research entries with their second review round,
  eight engineering calculators, all on phones and tablets.

## 5. Faster and smaller

- **Exports that scale:** the six-storey test building's report in about 10 seconds instead of four
  minutes, and a large model's results as a zip of CSV files instead of running out of memory.
- **The landing loads about 724 KB less and the editor about 215 KB less;** the course loads only
  when it is opened.
- **For contributors:** a change's full checks take about 22 minutes instead of six to eight hours.

## 6. Known limitations

- As in [0.4.0](v0.4.0.md#6-known-limitations).
- The open wrong-result issues contained by [#807](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/807) still give a warning or a refusal, not a
  fixed number: among them warped MITC4 and MITC9 shells ([#1057](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/1057)), MITC4 on distorted meshes
  ([#798](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/798)) and 2D plastic collapse ([#758](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/758)), which is refused.
- Torsion is not designed for ACI 318 or EN 1992 members; the CIRSOC 201 design checks it.
- The report check does not authenticate a report: a report rewritten in full with a new record
  still passes.
