# Stabileo 0.6.3: corrected seismic loads, member results and verification

Released 2026-11-20. These notes cover changes since **v0.6.2**. The full record is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Your results may change: re-run affected projects

For affected static seismic, panel-mode gravity and roof-step snow cases, **regenerate the loads first**, then rerun analyses, combinations, envelopes and dependent design checks. A solve with the old generated loads repeats their load placement. If generation is blocked, resolve the reported issue and regenerate the loads, or replace the saved loads with independently verified loads, before relying on the results. Replace modal seismic cases previously generated by Stabileo with static cases only where the static method applies. Where it cannot be used, follow the advisory before relying on the model.

Re-run affected detailed section stress queries, 2D moving-load sweeps and axial influence lines, and analyses that used explicit 2D self-weight rules or repeated load-case factors. Also rerun standalone analyses for saved projects that retain an imported gravity setting with self-weight turned off, even if they have no explicit 2D self-weight rules. Reload affected examples or fixtures from their original sources. Compare reactions, member forces, deflections, reinforcement, governing combinations and verification status with the values already used. Regenerate and review affected reports, workbooks and exports, including those that relied on the global end-moment signs from the 2D free-body inspector. Resolve refusals or obtain an independent check before using the affected result.

For braced frames, subdivided inclined members and sloped roofs with affected static seismic cases, compare story forces, base shear and rafter actions after regeneration.

Reimport affected workbooks from their source, verify coordinates, loads, gravity and restraints, then rerun the model. Re-export affected workbooks containing springs or restraints. Rerun affected concrete verification. Rerun CIRSOC 201 verification against the current reinforcement drawings and review governing combinations, verdicts and reports.

## Other changes

Static seismic generation blocks when no support datum can be established or when supports connected to participating seismic mass differ in elevation. Those supports determine the datum. Generation also blocks for disconnected geometry, participating mass below the supports, invalid shell geometry and unsupported transfers or accidental torsion. Modal load generation blocks before creating any cases. Modal modes, periods and participation remain available. Panel-mode gravity blocks when coplanar shell overlap or rigid links to an offset shell deck make coverage ambiguous. Review the model and resolve the reported conditions before regenerating loads.

Edits that could redirect a local roller or rotate a memberless 3D Z roller without a defined frame now refuse where the restraint cannot be preserved. Review the intended restraints in older snapshots. Non-finite support spring stiffnesses in the cable and soil–structure interaction web workflows and combinations whose nonzero factors cancel completely also refuse. A workbook with declared axes is rejected before it replaces the current model if it contains unsupported load or support components, ambiguous fields, or invalid prescribed rotations. Concrete verification refuses unsupported checks, gives the reason and issues no certificate for those checks. Detailed section stress still shows individual components when it cannot display combined stresses.

The global axes editor is **off by default**. When enabled, the selected axes govern the editor, model code, workbooks, drawings and reports while the solver uses the canonical coordinates.

The Terms of Use and Privacy Policy pages, the footer links to them, and the results disclaimer are now public. Sign-in is still disabled. [#2358](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2358)

Simplified Chinese is available throughout the app and public pages, with updated engineering explanations and recheck notices. Story drift controls remain reachable when a results warning is open. The 3D stress view stays responsive when hovering over supports, and slab-column point-joint notices remain visible when reinforcement verification is withheld. Large linear analyses and repeated concrete span checks do less work. The landing figures keep moving under the pointer, and signed-out project controls identify which actions require an account.

- Static seismic generation checks floor evidence at brace crossings and roof joints. A ridge beam alone cannot establish a floor. Straight member subdivisions are identified consistently after translation, and generation blocks if coordinate precision is insufficient or the story is ambiguous. Where both ends have floor evidence, elevated rafters receive distributed inertia along the participating spans. An established floor also splits a continuous inclined run at the floor joint. This can change story forces, base shear and rafter bending and deflection. [#2404](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2404)
- Panel gravity generation blocks a duplicate load path where an overlapping beam bay and shell deck would both be loaded and the deck seam is fully tied through off-seam master nodes. Automatic Loads also blocks before preview if coordinate roundoff makes a gravity carrier’s slope classification indeterminate. That classification could previously change after a plan rotation or member subdivision. On flat and pitched roofs, snow generation blocks when an on-site step drift needs transfer to members or its applicability cannot be established. Earlier transfer could lose force and eccentric moment. [#2406](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2406)
- Workbook import rejects sheet names that resolve to the same name, duplicate headers, missing required headers, invalid units, spreadsheet errors, conflicting restraint flags, and populated unsupported load fields. For planar loads, it rejects nonzero moment components about either of the two declared workbook axes that lie in the model plane. A refused import leaves the current model in place. Complete explicit restraints are preserved in the declared workbook axes, including in exports before solving. Planar rotational springs export as `kr [kN·m/rad]`, and the legacy header remains readable. [#2395](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2395)
- CIRSOC 201 verification uses concurrent axial force for stirrup spacing, checks anchorage at exact moment zeros and checks local stirrup intervals. Seismic hoop limits account for all end-bar layers. Torsion capacity and utilization reflect the lower evaluated limit. Checks can block for bars beyond their drawn ends or unverified perimeter-bar development. The concrete-only secondary shear check requires longitudinal bars seated in both upper stirrup bends. Final-depth rechecks use the replacement beam drawing when supplied. Column schedules retain mixed bar diameters and withhold physical detailing when the specified cage cannot be reproduced. [#2398](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2398)
- Clearing a plate mesh is faster, including when rebuilding slab meshes. 2D co-rotational analysis uses a shared nonlinear driver and retains its existing Newton and modified Newton results. [#2394](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2394)

## Known limitations

**Known wrong results remain open.** Read the affected scope and independently verify the relevant results before use.

- Static seismic generation can still lose actions in very thin mass bands or at nearly coincident torsion carriers. A legacy modal-load notice can also clear while the old loads remain. See [SA-2026-086](../advisories/SA-2026-086-seismic-static-story-forces.md) and [SA-2026-087](../advisories/SA-2026-087-seismic-modal-load-generation.md).
- Open findings remain for snow load transfer, concrete reinforcement, member-load recovery and restrained reactions. See the [product roadmap](../roadmap/PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md). Independently account for the disclosed one-sided snow torsion assumption. [#2224](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2224)
- **[SA-2026-056](../advisories/SA-2026-056-constrained-shell-dynamic-invariance.md) remains unfixed.** Independently verify dynamic demands for constrained mixed frame-and-shell models that change materially after renumbering, rotation or unit conversion. No conservative correction factor is established.
- 2D plastic collapse remains refused. The certified 3D lower bound applies only to the bound. Time history still requires independent verification of total dynamic support demands, including inertia, damping and support loading. [SA-2026-044](../advisories/SA-2026-044-plastic-2d-invariance.md)
- For the independent cable-tension check, include cable self-weight. [SA-2026-015](../advisories/SA-2026-015-cable-tension-chord-law.md)
