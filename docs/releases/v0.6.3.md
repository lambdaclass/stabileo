Released 2026-11-20. These notes cover changes since **v0.6.2**. The full record is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Your results may change: re-run affected projects

For affected static seismic and panel-mode gravity cases, **regenerate the loads first**, then rerun analyses, combinations, envelopes and dependent design checks. A solve with the old generated loads repeats their load placement. Replace previously generated modal seismic cases with static cases only where the static method applies. Where it cannot be used, follow the advisory before relying on the model.

Re-run affected concrete verification, detailed section stress queries, 2D moving-load sweeps and axial influence lines, and analyses that used explicit 2D self-weight rules or repeated load-case factors. Reimport affected workbooks and reload affected examples or fixtures from their original sources. Compare reactions, member forces, deflections, reinforcement, governing combinations and verification status with the values already used. Regenerate and review affected reports, workbooks and exports, including those that relied on the global end-moment signs from the 2D free-body inspector. Resolve refusals or obtain an independent check before using the affected result.

## Other changes

Static seismic generation blocks missing or stepped support datums, disconnected geometry, participating mass below the supports, invalid shell geometry and unsupported transfers or accidental torsion. Modal load generation blocks before creating any cases. Modal modes, periods and participation remain available. Panel-mode gravity blocks when coplanar shell overlap or rigid links to an offset shell deck make coverage ambiguous. Review the model and resolve the reported conditions before regenerating loads.

Edits that could redirect a local roller or rotate a memberless 3D Z roller without a defined frame now refuse where the restraint cannot be preserved. Review the intended restraints in older snapshots. Non-finite support spring stiffnesses in the cable and soil–structure interaction web workflows and combinations whose nonzero factors cancel completely also refuse. Declared workbooks with unsupported load or support components, ambiguous fields, or invalid prescribed rotations are refused before they replace the current model. Concrete verification withholds unsupported checks and certificates with a reason. Detailed section stress still shows individual components when a combined result is withheld.

The global axes editor is **off by default**. When enabled, the selected axes govern the editor, model code, workbooks, drawings and reports while the solver uses the canonical coordinates.

Unified PRO plates and the physical-model view are also **off by default**. With the switch enabled, existing shells become selectable plates, physical elements are recognized as the model changes, and analysis requires compatible plate meshes. Remeshing shares interface stations between neighbouring plates and preserves rectangular grids around openings. Coarse as-modelled plates are remeshed before analysis. If you used this option, rerun affected analyses and review reactions, displacements, member forces and shell stresses after remeshing. Reinforcement sheets remain disabled in app builds. [#2365](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2365)

Simplified Chinese is available throughout the app and public pages, with updated engineering explanations and recheck notices. Story drift controls remain reachable when a results warning is open. The 3D stress view stays responsive when hovering over supports, and slab-column point-joint notices remain visible when reinforcement verification is withheld. Large linear analyses and repeated concrete span checks do less work. The landing figures keep moving under the pointer, and signed-out project controls identify which actions require an account.

## Known limitations

**Known wrong results remain open.** Read the affected scope and independently verify the relevant results before use.

- Static seismic generation can still create fictitious stories at brace intersections or because of coordinate rounding, omit distributed rafter inertia on sloped roofs, or lose actions in very thin mass bands or nearly coincident torsion carriers. A legacy modal-load notice can also clear while the old loads remain. See [SA-2026-086](../advisories/SA-2026-086-seismic-static-story-forces.md) and [SA-2026-087](../advisories/SA-2026-087-seismic-modal-load-generation.md).
- Open findings remain for snow load transfer, concrete reinforcement, member-load recovery and restrained reactions. See the [product roadmap](../roadmap/PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md). Independently account for the disclosed one-sided snow torsion assumption. [#2224](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2224)
- **[SA-2026-056](../advisories/SA-2026-056-constrained-shell-dynamic-invariance.md) remains unfixed.** Independently verify dynamic demands for constrained mixed frame-and-shell models that change materially after renumbering, rotation or unit conversion. No conservative correction factor is established.
- 2D plastic collapse remains refused. The certified 3D lower bound applies only to the bound. Time history still requires independent verification of total dynamic support demands, including inertia, damping and support loading. [SA-2026-044](../advisories/SA-2026-044-plastic-2d-invariance.md)
- For the independent cable-tension check, include cable self-weight. [SA-2026-015](../advisories/SA-2026-015-cable-tension-chord-law.md)
