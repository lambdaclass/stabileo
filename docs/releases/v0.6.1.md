# Stabileo 0.6.1: corrected member checks and snow loads

**English** · [Español](v0.6.1.es.md) · [Português](v0.6.1.pt.md) · [Deutsch](v0.6.1.de.md) · [Français](v0.6.1.fr.md) · [Türkçe](v0.6.1.tr.md)

Released 2026-11-15. These notes cover changes since **v0.6.0**. The full record is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Your results may change: re-run affected projects

Re-run affected concrete and steel design checks. For generated snow, **regenerate the loads first**, then re-run affected load cases, combinations, envelopes and derived design checks. Re-solving previously saved loads alone does not repair them. Compare demands, capacities, reinforcement, utilization, governing combinations and certificates with results already used. Review delivered calculations and sizing based on them. A refusal, an unavailable quantity or unchecked reinforcement supplies no passing answer and is not zero demand.

## Safety advisories

The following five High-severity advisories are fixed in **0.6.1**. Follow each advisory for its affected inputs and user action, even if earlier results passed or were certified.

| Advisory | Severity | What changed and what to do |
|---|---|---|
| [SA-2026-064](../advisories/SA-2026-064-steel-cb-combination.md) | High | Steel Cb could come from another combination or an unlocated short unbraced span (#1316, #2088). Re-run CIRSOC 301 steel checks and review flexural capacity, interaction utilization and governing combinations. |
| [SA-2026-065](../advisories/SA-2026-065-snow-roof-step.md) | High | Roof-step drift could be omitted on a pitched receiving roof (#2082). Regenerate CIRSOC 104 snow loads and re-run affected analyses and design checks. |
| [SA-2026-066](../advisories/SA-2026-066-cirsoc201-transverse-cm.md) | High | Concrete Cm could receive end-moment credit with transverse load between supports (#2089). Re-check slender concrete columns and walls, magnified moments and reinforcement. |
| [SA-2026-067](../advisories/SA-2026-067-snow-roof-rotation.md) | High | Plan rotation could change generated snow loads (#2093). Regenerate loads using the actual roof geometry, then re-run analyses and checks; rotating an old model back is not a repair. |
| [SA-2026-068](../advisories/SA-2026-068-provided-torsion-stirrups.md) | High | Torsion could pass with inadequate designed stirrups (#2141). Re-run concrete design checks and review actual transverse capacity, stirrup spacing and torsion failures. Unchecked longitudinal provision is not a passing cage. |

## Other changes

Concrete and steel checks retain forces from the same combination and station. Incomplete combination or station data now refuses instead of borrowing an endpoint result. Required and provided reinforcement states remain separate, and missing longitudinal torsion provision is reported explicitly. Repeated identical design work is reused while every demand remains checked. Legacy-project comparisons now retain the released material and section defaults; saved projects and migrations are unchanged.

## Known limitations

- **Known wrong results remain open:** [#2096](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2096), [#2142](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2142), [#2143](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2143) and [#2144](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2144). This release does not fix them. Consult their affected scope and independently verify affected results before use.
- **[SA-2026-056](../advisories/SA-2026-056-constrained-shell-dynamic-invariance.md) remains unfixed:** independently verify dynamic demands for constrained mixed frame-and-shell models that change materially after renumbering, rotation or unit conversion. No conservative correction factor is established.
- 2D plastic collapse remains refused pending [#758](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/758), as described in [SA-2026-044](../advisories/SA-2026-044-plastic-2d-invariance.md). A certified 3D lower bound does not certify the raw hinge history.
- Time history does not provide total dynamic support demands. Use an independent analysis including inertia, damping and support loading. Unsupported cable analyses and nonzero prescribed support motion in cable/time-history workflows refuse explicitly.
- Spatial arc-length and displacement-control solvers are not added. Existing warnings and restrictions remain applicable.
