# Stabileo 0.6.4: Corrected member results, plate analysis and concrete verification

Released 2026-11-21. These notes cover changes since **v0.6.3**. The full record is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Your results may change: re-run affected projects

Reinspect affected Basic reaction moments and free-body readouts, and regenerate Basic and PRO reports that listed member point loads. Compare their signs and axes with equilibrium and the intended load directions.

Before solving, verify the original direction, loaded interval and end intensities of distributed loads on reversed members. Repair affected legacy planar point loads and check that each couple has the intended moment. Compare plate outlines and openings with their meshes, and repair stale meshes or incompatible wall–slab joints. Reload the corrected seven-storey gallery or architectural DXF example if you used either one. Rerun affected analyses, combinations and moving-load sweeps, then review governing member forces, displacements and design checks. Verify the concrete tension faces and drawn reinforcement, and rerun steel checks that used peak or quarter-point moments. Resolve refusals before relying on the results.

## Other changes

- Basic shows 3D support reaction moments with the solver’s global signs. Free-body readouts use the selected axes and match the node-side arrows in 2D. Basic and PRO report point-force and couple components in the solver’s plane and axes. Rerun affected inspections and regenerate reports. [#2396](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2396)

- PRO refuses analysis if an invalid or stale plate mesh remains or a wall–slab joint is incompatible. The mesher requires complete, valid outlines and holes before Apply. The updated seven-storey gallery and architectural DXF example have compatible junction meshes. New meshed CAD drafts align slab and wall stations. Model-only workbook exports can include unmeshed plates. [#2409](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2409)

- Member diagrams and moving-load envelopes recover peaks between stations, both sides of concentrated actions and the forces just inside member ends. Combined diagrams can show labelled conservative bounds when exact extrema are unavailable. Ambiguous stations, unresolved governing peaks and incomplete design station grids block the affected result. Reversed concrete span pieces retain the physical tension face. Steel checks use equilibrium peak and quarter-point moments and withhold moment-gradient credit when the required diagram information is incomplete. Reports with many stations reuse the prepared diagrams. [#2403](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2403)

- CIRSOC 201-2025 checks minimum transverse reinforcement for torsion in both directions and uses the evaluated concrete limit for pure torsion where a cage is available. Torsion and shear checks can refuse when the drawn cage lacks evidence for the perimeter bars, secondary effective depth or bar development across a joint. Shear checks use distinct tension bars present at the cut, including between sampled stations. The seven-storey example can now show refusals for checks that previously passed. [#2433](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2433)

- Both member reversal commands preserve the direction, loaded interval and end intensities of distributed loads. Analysis uses the unambiguous nonzero value in legacy planar couple fields and refuses conflicting values. In spatial models, a pure start couple can be applied at its authored node even without a usable member projection. Other legacy actions that still need the projection can block the solve. [#2456](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2456)

- A finite edit to a restored planar point couple or nodal moment replaces the stored moment, including when set to zero. Conflicting or invalid moments block analysis even after saving and reopening. Check the intended moment, re-enter it and rerun analysis and design. [#2467](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2467)

- Remeshing follows the curved boundary through the plate’s specified intermediate point. An ambiguous sweep blocks remeshing and analysis. Check the boundary, rebuild stale meshes and rerun analysis and design. Remeshing transfers shell offsets and curvature edits only to unique, exactly matching cells. If an edited shell cannot be matched, remeshing stops and preserves the existing mesh. Check shell properties against the intended model, restore any previously lost edits and rerun analysis and design. [#2466](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2466)

- The 3D reinforcement workspace opens faster, and saving, restoring and switching tabs in detailed models reuse immutable reinforcement geometry. Undo and redo restore detailing that matches the concrete geometry. [#2438](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2438), [#2444](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2444), [#2458](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2458)

- AI key settings give the provider buttons, key field and key actions a minimum size of 44 CSS pixels in both directions. [#2440](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2440)

- The published Mattiasson elastica, MacNeal–Harder and shell obstacle-course research pages show the first benchmark run’s completed result or its reported refusal. [#2445](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2445)

- The product roadmap prioritizes independent load and plate-area checks, exact internal-force functions, complete design evidence and one canonical model with checks across transformations. [#2469](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/pull/2469)

## Known limitations

**Known wrong results remain open.** Read the affected scope and independently verify the relevant results before use.

- Static seismic generation can still lose actions in very thin mass bands or at nearly coincident torsion carriers. A legacy modal-load notice can also clear while the old loads remain. See [SA-2026-086](../advisories/SA-2026-086-seismic-static-story-forces.md) and [SA-2026-087](../advisories/SA-2026-087-seismic-modal-load-generation.md).
- Open findings remain for snow load transfer, concrete reinforcement, member-load recovery and restrained reactions. See the [product roadmap](../roadmap/PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md). Independently account for the disclosed one-sided snow torsion assumption. [#2224](https://github.com/lambdaclass/stabileo/issues/2224)
- **[SA-2026-056](../advisories/SA-2026-056-constrained-shell-dynamic-invariance.md) remains unfixed.** Independently verify dynamic demands for constrained mixed frame-and-shell models that change materially after renumbering, rotation or unit conversion. No conservative correction factor is established.
- 2D plastic collapse remains refused. The certified 3D lower bound applies only to the bound. Time history still requires independent verification of total dynamic support demands, including inertia, damping and support loading. [SA-2026-044](../advisories/SA-2026-044-plastic-2d-invariance.md)
- For the independent cable-tension check, include cable self-weight. [SA-2026-015](../advisories/SA-2026-015-cable-tension-chord-law.md)
