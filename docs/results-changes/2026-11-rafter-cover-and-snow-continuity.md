# Rafter-gap cover and snow roof continuity

## Include certified rafter gaps in deck exposure

A certified gap carries area load on its attached roof plane. Exposure uses that footprint when evaluating cover over lower decks, including other certified gaps. The upper deck retains its own roof load. Partial shelter still requires uniform pressure or an explicit assignment.

## Follow continuous snow roof seams and opening edges

Modeled step drift includes a coplanar bay across an exact short seam. Snow roof grouping checks both exterior and opening edges for continuous joins. An actual open seam still interrupts fetch. Snow drift on an unsupported receiving panel continues to use the translated transfer refusal.
