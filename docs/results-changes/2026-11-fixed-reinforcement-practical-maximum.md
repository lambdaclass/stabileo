# Practical maximum retained for fixed reinforcement (2026-11-14)

PR #2080 round 13 fixes a practical-limit bypass in the fixed-reinforcement path reviewed at `7dcb1176ed`.

`checkFlexure` rejects a required steel area exceeding its stated practical maximum, `0.04*b*h*1e4` in cm². This is the existing practical ceiling, not a new code-clause limit. `prepareElementVerification` replaced that result's status when checking a fixed cage, considering minimum steel, capacity and ductility but omitting the practical maximum. `verifyConcurrentElement` always checks a fixed shared cage, so it could return an accepted member whose independent design explicitly failed for requiring too much steel.

Both independent and fixed-cage flexure construction now pass their final result through `enforcePracticalFlexureLimit`. It checks the final required area against the result's maximum, sets failure and appends the same explanation. There is one limit decision and one explanation formatter. Changing a cage cannot bypass this final check. Fixed-cage minimum-steel recomputation remains in place. The ceiling concerns required steel; this change does not introduce a new limit on rounded provided area or on compression steel.

The [numerical witness](2080-round13-practical-maximum-diff.json) records exact parent/current/main source provenance and before/after fields. The beam has b=0.2 m, h=0.5 m, cover=0.025 m, stirrups=8 mm, fc=25 MPa, fy=420 MPa and zero axial and shear demand. At Mu=800 kN·m the maximum is 40 cm². Required steel is 57.7196536019777 cm² for 2005 and 51.72478327289504 cm² for 2025. Both independent results already failed. Fixed and concurrent checks previously returned `ok` for 2005 and `warn` for 2025, without the practical-limit explanation. They now return `fail`, retain the explanation and emit `VERIF_FAIL_FLEXURE`. Capacity and demand/capacity ratio are unchanged. At Mu=150 kN·m both editions retain their prior reinforcement, capacity and accepted warning status.

Tests explicitly enabled on reproducer commit A against parent production code yielded 94 failures and 40 passing controls. They cover both editions, both moment axes, beams/columns/walls, direct/prepared/concurrent and aggregate/station results, both sides of the practical threshold, and accepted ordinary inputs. Real planar and spatial WASM analysis results exercise linear and P-Delta models, endpoint and station extraction, compact/grouped transfer, auto verification, unified verification and the design wrapper. The actual 2D WASM solve and P-Delta exports also supply force tuples to direct, prepared and concurrent checking. The changed designer is TypeScript orchestration; no separate native fixed-cage designer exists. No Rust numerical kernel or solver formula changed.

The CI recheck test's extra notice was `complete-concurrent-demands`, introduced in round 12. Its mixed frame contains concrete and steel members, so that notice's `concreteMembers OR steelMembers` applicability is correct. The exact expected lists now include it. They also explicitly include this change's concrete practical-maximum notice, with both SSI registry variants retained. No applicability rule or assertion was relaxed.
