# Concrete shear, torsion and seismic hoop verification refusals

Previously passing CIRSOC 201 designs can now return a blocking verification result. The shear, torsion and seismic capacity formulas continue to use the existing design equations.

- In #2310, the fixed beam subjected to combined shear, torsion and axial tension reported torsion OK at a utilization of 0.9701335. Fixed and station verification now withhold approval when the combined section limit would pass using a concrete shear contribution that the evaluated cage cannot substantiate. The fixed and station checks also block combined torsion where they would overstate primary effective depth. Beam longitudinal torsion requires evidence of the bars around the closed cage. Fixed beam and wall checks refuse active longitudinal torsion when the only declaration is a scalar steel area. In the slab-mesh span trace, the solved slab torque activated this check in both spans. Verification now refuses those cages.
- In #2300, a longitudinal torsion row credited 6.03 cm² at a cut beyond the top bar terminus and reported OK at 0.507. A longitudinal torsion row now blocks if its credited area exceeds the area that reaches the cut. Perimeter spacing and area use the bars that reach the same cut. Continuing inner rows cannot replace terminated corner bars. A beam with continuous stirrups and a corner-bar gap between the sampled sections also reported OK. Drawn bar termini and group cutoffs now add cuts just inside the adjoining intervals. Verification interpolates concurrent forces within each combination at those cuts and refuses active torsion when the bar evidence is insufficient.
- In #2301, the published cage already retains its two primary legs on main. When the existing detailing rule requires more legs than supplied, fixed verification now gives a translated refusal while retaining the declared leg count and the strength evaluated with it.
- In #2302, the seismic hoop length check passed using the 1 m declared region even though the reopened drawing ended the dense end cage at 0.659 m. The check now measures contiguous matching hoops inward from both support faces in the actual drawing, using the same projection that opens a legacy line.
- In #2309, the 2005 section checker could pass active torsion using uncapped 500 MPa steel. It now refuses active torsion when the specified yield exceeds the 420 MPa cap in §11.6.3.4. The station path already applies the cap.
- In #2296, an active cut in a drawn stirrup gap reported 343.10 kN of shear capacity. Verification now checks gaps at sampled cuts and between them under either shear component or active torsion, including secondary shear with zero torque. A hogging cut that uses the larger span depth also blocks when the reinforcement indicates a shallower tension face.

The automatic design search selects reinforcement and settles crossing seats before the shear-depth containment can affect certification. When the selected cage has unsupported effective depth, design withholds its certificate and rechecks surviving selections against the resulting seats without reopening failed searches. On the 408-member flagship, 82 formerly certified beams now refuse on shear depth. In that comparison, the other 326 member fingerprints matched the previous record exactly, including every certified utilization retained by containment. The [settled-seat verification follow-up](2026-11-rc-settled-seat-certification.md) records the additional findings.

Re-run affected concrete member verification and review any blocked checks before relying on the design or detailing.
