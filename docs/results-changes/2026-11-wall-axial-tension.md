# Signed axial force in concrete wall design

## Wall axial tension

Floor design could turn a tensile membrane resultant into zero before checking the wall and recording its demand. Reopen affected projects, rerun wall design and regenerate drawings and reports. The prior OK status and prior reinforcement may change.

The floor and CIRSOC wall sheet check the signed force against the vertical bars. A pure tensile check uses the steel area and yield strength under §22.4.3.1. With bending, the member check evaluates the moment capacity at the applied axial force. With shear, net tension reduces the concrete contribution under CIRSOC 201-2025 §11.5.4.4. The wall assembly places the vertical bars counted by the section check. A floor check requiring an unmodelled net section at an opening or an unsupported edition-specific shear check blocks the run.
