# Selection of the inward footing for a strap

## Review short off-axis foundation straps

The integrated foundation generator could propose a nearby point outside the allowed inward direction by applying the edge tolerance for a wide pier to a footing with no such width. If the proposal was placed, the model contains a different foundation beam and strap endpoint.

Review the chosen partner of a short off-axis strap. Correct any misplaced beam and footing connection and rerun the analysis and foundation checks. Point-footing proposals now use the released direction and distance criteria. A sheet pier whose width is explicitly positive retains its wider selection.
