# Preserve extrema at member section cuts

Two complementary triangular loads with peak intensity 2⁵⁴ cancel a uniform
load of −2⁵⁴. A coincident narrow-offset ramp from +1 to −1 on a unit
member has a shear peak of one quarter of its loaded length. Forming the
complement of the local fraction before accumulating the intensity terms can
hide that peak. The diagram evaluator adds the start intensity and the two
local-fraction terms independently.

If forward projection rounds an extremum onto the right boundary, the diagram also projects it from that boundary.
Transfer uses the ordinate change about the source station, preserving the
local step through cancellation. In moving-load envelopes, a concentrated
J-end jump triggers direct evaluation of the inward cut.

Spatial resultants evaluate all components at the original piece-local cut.
An ABS combination provides its signed polynomial on each sign interval, so
the resultant can include extrema between component stations. Critical force
rows retain the left and right values of jumps. Variable-member envelopes
refuse distinct jump cuts with a shared normalized label.

Recompute affected member diagrams and envelopes and regenerate their reports.
