# Gravity generation requires unambiguous shell coverage

Panel-mode gravity generation blocks when two load-bearing shell footprints overlap in the same plane, or when an offset shell is rigidly connected to a beam panel that would receive gravity over the same area. The check follows rigid links that tie vertical translation, including links through a common master. It checks all faces connected by shared mesh nodes or coincident nodes tied in all six degrees of freedom, including faces across a seam tied through a common master. Contact at a footprint edge alone still permits both loads. A shell with an independent load path at a separate level remains eligible for its own pressure.

On main at `23fc68ff7`, the 10 × 6 m bay in #2284 reports 72 kN of superimposed dead load under 1 kN/m². The two shell footprints cover 24 m² each and share 12 m². The bay has a physical area of 60 m², so the overlap accounts for the extra 12 kN. The #2299 witness reports 120 kN for the same bay and pressure, with 60 kN applied to the shell and 60 kN applied directly to its rigidly connected beam ring. The single loaded surface accounts for 60 kN. Using a common master for each beam and shell corner pair also reports 120 kN on that revision. Attaching the beam ring to the exterior strip of a continuous 12 × 6 m shell deck reports 132 kN, including 60 kN applied twice over the bay. On main at `09c800543`, the same exterior strip still reports 132 kN when its coincident seam nodes are duplicated and fully tied, either directly or through coincident masters. The models now return a translated blocking reason before producing a load plan.

Regenerate gravity in potentially affected panel-mode projects. If generation blocks, establish the physical carriers and their load regions before relying on analysis or design results. The pressure and tributary transfer formulas for accepted models are unchanged.
