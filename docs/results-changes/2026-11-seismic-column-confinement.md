# Recorded axial demand for seismic column confinement

A primary concrete frame column could report adequate confinement using a zero axial force when no eligible station supplied an axial range, or omit the axial force when passing the column to the tie check. The frame now withholds the confinement check when any active seismic combination lacks finite axial and shear station results, when only gravity combinations are available, or the demand falls in the range where the detailed method needs the count and arrangement of held perimeter bars. Automatic detailing skips that column. Review existing frame reports and verify the tie layout before issuing reinforcement details.

A primary beam or column without a reinforcement record, or a primary column without its reinforced section, now carries a blocking report issue. Review its section and reinforcement before issuing details. Secondary members remain outside this primary-member check.

The results notice covers 3D concrete frame projects with saved reinforcement, seismic design settings or a combination using an earthquake case. A gravity-only model without reinforcement or a recorded seismic setup has no saved evidence of this check. Solved station forces are reused without changing the solver.
