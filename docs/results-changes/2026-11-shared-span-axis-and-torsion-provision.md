# Shared beam axes and explicit longitudinal torsion provision (2026-11-15)

PR #2080 round 22 corrects two defects introduced on the unmerged branch. The shared-span cache introduced by `7f24f74f25` compared the rotated section dimensions and material inputs but excluded the complete section demand. Two beams sharing the same concurrent tuples, with unequal My/Mz and reversed rectangular dimensions, can select opposite axes while presenting identical rotated b/h. The second beam then inherited the first beam's flexural and shear demands, reinforcement, capacities and certificate. Reuse now compares every own verification parameter except the member ID, plus every own field of each immutable section demand, for every station. The axis and source demand therefore participate automatically, including future input fields. Member outputs remain independent.

The torsion summary assigned the maximum required longitudinal steel to `AlProv`. Direct verification also defaulted missing provision to its own requirement. This fabricated an always-sufficient longitudinal cage. Exact origin/main `013485aa2629a05672403919060cc1397f510fd1` reports `AlReq` without a longitudinal provision check. This branch now also keeps requirement separate from provision: no supplied area means `longitudinalProvision: not-checked`, an explicit warning and calculation step, a held normalized member result, and no passing certificate. Transverse reinforcement, spacing and section checks still run and retain proven failures. Explicitly supplied longitudinal provision is checked at the unchanged boundary. Negligible and zero torque retain their answered controls. No longitudinal bar layout is invented from a required area.

The enabled initial reproducer matrix fails 49 tests on unchanged starting production and passes 156 controls. It covers opposite axes through auto, unified and design entries, both editions and element orders, missing longitudinal provision through direct/prepared/concurrent entries for beams, columns and walls on both axes, and the duplicated steel work. Further tests exercise actual linear and P-Delta WASM outputs in 2D and 3D, exact cached-versus-isolated member equality on the shared-span building fixtures, explicit longitudinal provision immediately below/at/above its requirement, neglect and spacing boundaries, and batched/scalar steel equality across batch boundaries. The orchestration changes are TypeScript-only; no native numerical solver or material/design formula changes.

Concurrent steel checks also avoid repeated work: whole-member checks are batched, and their already-computed interaction ratios rank the governing station. The governing interaction retains its traced engine check and the existing certified gate. Each scalar and batch entry shares one preparation and conversion path. Optimizer screening honors its existing trace-off option and omits calculation rendering while retaining every engineering field. Every station remains checked; no demand, tolerance, timeout, baseline or numerical assertion is relaxed.
