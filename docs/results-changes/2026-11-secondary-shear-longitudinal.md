# CIRSOC 201 secondary shear uses the longitudinal layout (#2171)

Secondary shear capacity and combined shear and torsion now use the same secondary effective depth. The check subtracts clear cover, actual stirrup diameter and half the largest longitudinal bar diameter from the full depth of the secondary face. A rectangular section with a 300 mm secondary depth, 25 mm cover, Ø8 stirrups and Ø32 longitudinal bars gives an effective depth of 251 mm. The previous 259 mm depth overestimated capacity and understated shear stress. For the 300 × 600 mm CIRSOC 201-2005 test column with two secondary Ø8 legs at 100 mm, the change reduces the design shear capacity from 179.19951 kN to 173.66439 kN. The verifier reports a refusal when the needed longitudinal bar diameter is unknown. [CIRSOC 201-2005 Chapter 11](https://app.inti.gob.ar/greenstone3/biblio/collection/works/document/ReglamentoCIRSOC201-2005_pdf), [CIRSOC 201-2025 §§22.5 and 22.7.7.1(a)](../codes/CIRSOC/markdown/cirsoc-201-2025/capitulo-22-resistencia-de-las-secciones-de-los-elementos.md#c22.7.7.1).

For a rectangular column, fixed, prepared and automatic verification now use the station verifier’s tension ratio of one third of the selected longitudinal steel area divided by the gross section area. This affects the concrete contribution below the 2025 transverse minimum and the shear steel reserved in the torsion check. In the 550 × 600 mm test section with three secondary Ø6 legs at 250 mm, zero axial force and a 511 mm effective depth, four Ø16 bars give a design concrete contribution of 57.3870 kN and eight Ø16 bars give 72.3181 kN. The fixed verifier previously gave zero in this case. Verification includes demands from 0.001 kN in each path. [CIRSOC 201-2025 Table 22.5.5.1](../codes/CIRSOC/markdown/cirsoc-201-2025/capitulo-22-resistencia-de-las-secciones-de-los-elementos.md).

The row-2 design snapshot records secondary shear capacities of 328.4576131 kN on columns 1–4, down from 329.8950644 kN in the preceding implementation. The RC QA diagnostic snapshot records updated secondary shear capacities and ratios on beams 13–20. Re-run affected spatial concrete designs and review their secondary shear and torsion checks.
