# Property-line anchors for new foundations

## Review footing placement near property lines

An integration build could read property boundaries from saved reinforcement-sheet settings while the sheet tools were disabled. Generating isolated footings or pile caps could then anchor a foundation to an unselected boundary. A column with a wider section could also receive an edge anchor when only its face reached the perimeter of the other columns.

With sheets disabled, the placement form uses its current boundary selections and the extreme support axes, as in the released tool. The sheet workflow retains its saved boundaries and includes columns whose faces reach a selected boundary. Compare the positions and anchors of foundations previously generated in affected builds with the intended site boundaries. A saved footing retains its authored anchor when opened. Recheck affected footing or strap designs after correcting the model.
