# Frame reinforcement status in mixed RC drawings

The reinforcement workspace could label a bare beam or column as modelled when a slab, wall or footing owned bars with the same numerical identifier. On the seven-storey regression model, after designing one exterior frame and the selected beams, 56 bare columns were reported as modelled. Each of those columns had only family-owned bars matching its number.

The status report now matches the kind of owner as well as its number. A bare member with no design outcome reads as not evaluated. A verified member without drawn steel reads as designed but not modelled. When a footing and a column share a number, each has its own status and can be inspected separately. Review previously issued member status summaries for mixed frame and floor designs. The change affects the displayed classifications and their counts. The analysis forces, designed bars and verification certificates are unchanged.
