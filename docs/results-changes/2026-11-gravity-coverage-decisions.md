# Preserve gravity on valid deck and roof geometry

Panel gravity intersects shells with the bay area after excluding openings. A shell filling a 4 m² framed opening in a 60 m² floor receives 4 kN of shell pressure at 1 kN/m², and the beam bay transfers the other 56 kN. A shell that partly occupies an adjacent bay still requires resolved ownership. Generation stops if a retained low roof fringe would reclaim a shell-filled opening.

Each connected overlap must clear the existing vertical separation limit. A shell can pass above one arm of a U-shaped bay and below the other when its intersection with the bay elevation lies entirely in the gap. Both carriers retain their gravity loads.

Panel and width generation use the member endpoints to establish whether its midpoint lies on a collinear roof edge, including when one endpoint extends beyond that edge. The lower member then receives the same coverage classification for gravity and snow. A rejected wide ring blocks generation when it could change a coverage decision. The industrial hall and the beam-only stadium test models keep their existing load classifications.

Regenerate automatic loads on affected projects before using analysis or design results.
