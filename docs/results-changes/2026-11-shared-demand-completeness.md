# Shared demand completeness before member design (2026-11-15)

Issue #2096 concerns a member verdict produced from a subset of the requested concurrent demands. The reviewed main is `8e5c026b7125f920c7a29141aa375c98c178f9b3`. It already refuses missing combination results and empty foreign-code tuple sets in several APIs, following #2080. Those checks do not establish every declared station or every eligible model member. The EC2 peak designer also combines whatever demands remain. Two foreign-code callers further shrink the active combination inventory to the results that exist before dispatch.

`CompleteDemandSet.require` is the single completeness check. It compares a scope's combinations, members and declared stations with the assembled rows. It throws `IncompleteConcurrentDemandError` with discriminated diagnostic codes and combination/member/station identities, or produces an opaque complete set. The numerical dispatcher and certification cores, CIRSOC aggregation and steel-check core require that type. Concurrent point demands also require a complete-set proof in their private constructor. Station extraction records the declared inventory; axial masking, interpolation and tuple conversion preserve it. Reassembling a subset cannot erase that inventory. Missing force records are checked against model members before result iteration.

The same check guards compact and grouped station transfer, foreign member contexts, all five dispatched codes, direct and prepared CIRSOC station input, CIRSOC auto/unified/design verification, aggregation, supplied reinforcement, CIRSOC steel and profile searches, variable-section steel, and EC2 member design. Foreign-code store and panel callers pass the full active inventory. A single-result fallback cannot replace requested combinations. Design tables and reports reach these extraction/verification paths; existing saved verdicts receive the result-change notice.
