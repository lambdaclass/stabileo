`CompleteDemandSet.require` is the single completeness check. It compares a scope's combinations, members and declared stations with the assembled rows. It throws `IncompleteConcurrentDemandError` with discriminated diagnostic codes and combination/member/station identities, or produces an opaque complete set. The numerical dispatcher and certification cores, CIRSOC aggregation and steel-check core require that type. Concurrent point demands also require a complete-set proof in their private constructor. Station extraction records the declared inventory; axial masking, interpolation and tuple conversion preserve it. Reassembling a subset cannot erase that inventory. Missing force records are checked against model members before result iteration.
