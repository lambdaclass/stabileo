# CIRSOC 201 shear and torsion use the evaluated cage (#2171)

Fixed torsion verification now reserves transverse steel for shear using the concrete strength of the evaluated stirrups. For the 450 × 320 mm, 25 MPa beam witness, the design concrete contribution is 56.27 kN just below the transverse minimum and 80.61 kN just above it. The sizing trial previously supplied 80.61 kN on both sides of the limit. The section torsion check also requires a resolved secondary face at active torque with concurrent secondary shear. The station sweep reports unresolved faces even when another evaluated cage has a finite capacity. [CIRSOC 201-2025 Table 22.5.5.1 and §22.7.7.1](../codes/CIRSOC/markdown/cirsoc-201-2025/capitulo-22-resistencia-de-las-secciones-de-los-elementos.md).

The torsion sweep compares demand to the edition’s rectangular-section threshold at full precision. The station verifier can check a wall's transverse torsion strength and will withhold verification until the longitudinal torsion steel is established around the perimeter. Top and bottom steel area alone cannot establish whether the longitudinal steel meets the distribution requirements. [CIRSOC 201-2005 §§11.6.1 and 11.6.6.2](https://app.inti.gob.ar/greenstone3/biblio/collection/works/document/ReglamentoCIRSOC201-2005_pdf), [CIRSOC 201-2025 §§9.7.5 and 22.7.4.1](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/420000-424999/422490/res11-4.pdf).

The beam verification credits concrete alone on the secondary axis. Rectangular columns use Table 10.7.6.5.2 for along-member spacing.
