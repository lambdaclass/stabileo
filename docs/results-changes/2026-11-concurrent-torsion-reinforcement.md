# Concurrent torsion reinforcement retained across cases (2026-11-14)

PR #2080 round 14 fixes reinforcement lost when aggregating concurrent concrete verification results at `3ff2a10021`.

The aggregate torsion row was copied from the case with the worst status and utilization. Torsion utilization measures the section limit under the concurrent shear, or the neglect threshold for a negligible torque. Neither orders the required reinforcement: another case can require larger At/s and longitudinal Al. Selecting a utilization governor therefore discarded real reinforcement requirements. The concurrent checker also supplied only a shared shear cage, without checking its combined shear and torsion steel or a shared longitudinal torsion allocation.

`sharedTorsionReinforcement` collects maximum At/s and Al from every non-negligible preliminary case independently of utilization. It sizes common stirrup spacing against the existing shear requirement plus twice the torsion requirement, each outer leg's requirement, and the torsion module's spacing limit. It retains the shear designer's existing minimum proposed spacing; insufficient provided steel fails rather than gaining credit for an unprovided arrangement. Both shear axes use the same stirrups. The prepared verification closure checks the fixed longitudinal torsion allocation and the outer-leg steel remaining after each case's shear requirement. Direct and prepared fixed verification share this check. Both sizing and verification obtain actual stirrup provision from the existing torsion module, which uses the bar diameter; they do not introduce another bar-area formula. All concurrent cases receive the same longitudinal allocation and actual stirrups.

The summary keeps the original governing utilization, torque, capacity and combination. Its At/s and Al fields are maxima across cases, and `governingCombos.torsionReinforcement` identifies their governing case. `neglect` describes the member: it is true only if every case permits neglect. `AtOverSProv` in the summary is the steel available in the reinforcement-governing case; each concurrent result records its own available steel after shear. `reinforcementRatio` in the summary is the largest steel/spacing utilization across cases. The existing `ratio` remains the governing section/neglect utilization. Calculation steps explicitly distinguish the governing case from the maxima over all combinations. The engine's existing torsion-not-rechecked disclosure remains.

The [numerical witness](2080-round14-torsion-diff.json) records the exact parent, current and main results and source hashes. The beam uses b=0.3 m, h=0.6 m, fc=25 MPa, fy=420 MPa, cover=0.025 m and 8 mm stirrups. C1 has V=300 kN and T=5 kN·m; C2 has V=0 and T=30 kN·m. Both have M=20 kN·m and zero axial force. In both editions the parent accepts the member but reports negligible torsion and zero At/s and Al. C1's utilization is 0.897867564534231; C2 requires At/s=4.271172651305651 cm²/m and Al=6.69719871724726 cm². The corrected summary retains these requirements and marks torsion non-negligible. Shared stirrups at 0.075 m provide 6.702064327658225 cm²/m in the reinforcement-governing case; the shared additional longitudinal allocation equals the maximum Al. Every case is checked, and this witness remains accepted. Underprovided fixed transverse or longitudinal steel now fails. Zero-torsion controls retain their answer.
