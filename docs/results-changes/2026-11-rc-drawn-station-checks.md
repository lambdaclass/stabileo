# RC beam strength at drawn cage transitions and bar cutoffs

A beam line can have a weaker stirrup zone entirely between two sampled force stations. Its regional projection can miss the zone. Verification now uses the drawn diameter, leg count and spacing wherever the drawing supplies cage evidence. It evaluates the force tuple at each boundary with the cages on both sides. The same drawn cage lookup feeds primary shear, secondary shear and torsion.

An interpolated bar cutoff can govern coupled shear and torsion even when the sampled sections pass. The cutoff goes through the same torsion sweep as a sampled section and can produce the governing depth or concrete refusal. When active torsion requires perimeter steel, every credited bar must meet the minimum diameter from CIRSOC 201-05 §11.6.6.2 or CIRSOC 201-25 §9.7.5.2, including the requirement based on actual hoop spacing. [CIRSOC 201-05, INTI](https://app.inti.gob.ar/greenstone3/biblio/collection/works/document/ReglamentoCIRSOC201-2005_pdf) · [CIRSOC 201-25, InfoLEG](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/420000-424999/422490/res11-4.pdf)
