The four-leg cage reports 138.08 kN of design concrete strength where the trial reported zero.

The station verifier credits a corner bar on each lateral tension face where the outer row and its continuation establish the bar's presence. The two lateral signs read the same bound because the credited corner pairs are symmetric. The check uses the seating geometry and the commentary's outer-third criterion for the credited steel. A mixed outer row or an incomplete continuation supplies no corner credit from that layer. The verifier uses the least effective depth of the credited corners. Lateral flexure and arbitrary bar positions require their own verification.

Primary shear and torsion use the station's concurrent primary bending sign to select the same tension geometry. This extends the [round 28 support shear correction](2198-round28-shear-geometry.md) to the shear–torsion interaction and the longitudinal torsion demand.
