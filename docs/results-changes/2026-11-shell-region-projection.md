# Shell pressure regions are checked in their authored projection

A region extrudes its footprint along the specified normal. A shell can lie partly or wholly inside that extrusion even when the projection collapses the shell footprint to a line. The assembler omitted clipped loads on such shells. The independent audit also expected zero for an exactly singular projection, so an analysis could succeed with the load missing. The same omission could reduce the mass taken from the load case.

The audit now intersects the shell and the loaded region, with the openings removed, in the plane perpendicular to the region direction. It lifts a regular intersection back to the shell for force, moment and corner-force checks. An edge-on or nearly edge-on shell that meets the loaded region is refused with the load case and shell identifier. A disjoint footprint, a footprint strictly inside an opening and identically zero projected pressure can still give a zero result. Boundary contact and coverage that depends on the union of multiple openings are treated conservatively when the projection is singular.

The review fixture is a fully restrained 2 m × 2 m vertical shell within a horizontal load region. With a global downward pressure of 3 kN/m², its total support reaction should be 12 kN. The previous analysis returned zero. The equivalent regular projection just above the assembly cutoff gives 12 kN and passes the audit. Previously analysed projects with affected surface loads should be checked and solved again after the load extent is defined in a projection that can be assembled.
