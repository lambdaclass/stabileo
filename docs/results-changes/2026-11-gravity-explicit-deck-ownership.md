# Require unambiguous automatic gravity ownership

Automatic panel gravity assigns a beam bay to shells only when the shell geometry proves that the same physical deck covers the entire bay. The check accepts coplanar shared boundaries and exact coincidence. The original rigidly attached and eccentric shell-bay examples still use the shell when all boundary stations identify the same planar deck at a uniform offset. Separate carriers must clear the existing deck separation limit throughout their overlap.

A shell rising from 3.02 m to 3.06 m over a 60 m² bay at 3.00 m formerly received full pressure alongside the bay. A second branch example reassigned bay pressure after discarding its lower shell in favour of a higher shell. Both now refuse before automatic loads are generated. Partial footprints, shells in bay openings and overlapping shells with unresolved ownership also refuse. A rejected wide beam ring cannot supply overhead cover. If its presence would affect a covered point, generation requires an explicit deck or explicit loads.

Regenerate affected automatic loads after resolving the geometry or assign their loads explicitly. Existing stored loads do not change until regeneration.
