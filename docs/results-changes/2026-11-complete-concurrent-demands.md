# Complete concurrent results and distinct reinforcement states (2026-11-14)

PR #2080 round 12 fixes the incomplete-result refusals and concrete result coherence reviewed at `ffba33bb4e`.

`ConcurrentDemand.fromStations` previously discarded combinations with empty stations. Its consumer could verify and certify the remaining subset. The foreign-code dispatcher and its certified path also allowed governing force peaks to stand in for missing or empty full-station tuples. Both now use `hasCompleteConcurrentDemand`: there must be at least one result group and every expected combination must have station data. Foreign-code checking and certification use the same tuple grouping, including combination ids present in the governing demands. Incomplete members are skipped or uncertified with a translated reason; no engine check runs for them. Empty tuple conversion remains an empty utility result; it cannot pass either dispatch boundary.

Both compact and grouped WASM station transfers can omit a member missing from one combination. The shared adapter retains the expected per-combination inventory and represents an absent member force record or absent station group as empty. Extraction then reaches the same refusal. A legitimately zero-force station is present data and remains valid. This guard does not infer combinations absent from every supplied inventory; callers must supply the active analysis results.

Round 8 (`823da327c`) changed `d` to the provided-bars depth; round 11 changed the separate workbook fixture, not this calculation. The expectation and its original tolerance are retained. Rectangular and flanged independent sizing and shared-cage checks now derive minimum steel from the provided depth through the existing minimum-steel function. Selection verifies this minimum too; a shared cage below it fails. `AsReq` remains the maximum of strength-required steel and that minimum, while `designDepth` preserves the strength sizing solution.

Shared-cage checking overwrote `epsilonT` with provided-steel strain but retained required-solution `c`, `aReq` and `designDepth`. It now preserves all required-solution fields. The typed `providedFlexureState` projection, shared by independent and fixed-cage outputs, exposes the evaluated block depth and `epsilonTProv` and cannot return the required strain field.
