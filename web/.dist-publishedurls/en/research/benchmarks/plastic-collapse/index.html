Classic plastic collapse

After M. R. Horne,“Plastic Theory of Structures”, Pergamon Press (1979), doi:10.1016/c2013-0-05820-3.

Against the hand calculation
✓ 6 of 6 checks pass· see the checks
Against the paper’s figures
– No figure or table from the book is used: the example and its dimensions are ours, so the hand calculation is the reference.

Research, not a design tool. Nothing here is an engineering result.

One fixed-base portal, three hand-calculated mechanisms, and the production 3D plastic-collapse solver.

Span L = 4 m, height h = 3 m, uniform Mp = 100 kN·m. Both bases are fixed. The roof carries λH = λ × 1 kN to the right at the left corner and λV = λ × 2 kN down at midspan. The beam is split at the load. The frame lies in the XZ plane; no out-of-plane load is applied.

Virtual work with rigid members and small rotations gives the three upper bounds below. The combined mechanism cancels the left roof hinge: internal work is 6Mpθ, external work is λ(Hh + VL/2)θ. Its minimum governs. Axial deformation is elastic; axial and shear yielding, hardening and geometric instability are outside this hand calculation. Combined →

  • Beam: λ = 8Mp / (VL) = 100.000000
  • Sway: λ = 4Mp / (Hh) = 133.333333
  • Combined: λ = 6Mp / (Hh + VL/2) = 85.714286

The three bounds are our own hand derivations with the virtual-work mechanism method, the method of Horne, Plastic Theory of Structures (1979), which we cite as background reading: we have not checked a page or equation number in the book. The dimensions are ours; no figure or worked example from the book is reproduced. Crossref gives the title and year without authors; the publisher Elsevier identifies M. R. Horne.

Checks

  • ✓ The collapse factor equals the combined bound 6Mp / (Hh + VL/2) within 1 × 10⁻⁶. passes
  • ✓ The solver reports a complete mechanism and no warnings. passes
  • ✓ Hinges form at both bases, at midspan and at the right roof corner; the left roof corner never yields. passes
  • ✓ At collapse |My| ≤ Mp at every member end. passes
  • ✓ The reactions balance the applied loads: ΣFx, ΣFz and ΣMy within 1 × 10⁻⁹. passes
  • ✓ By statics the left roof corner carries 3Mp − λHh = 42.857143 kN·m, and the beam equation 3Mp + M = λVL/2 holds. passes

Scope

  • Not a design check.
  • One load ratio and one uniform section; no axial–moment interaction, buckling or large displacements.
  • The 2D plastic solver is not used here; this page runs the 3D solver (see issue #758).
  • The viewer joins the displaced nodes with straight lines and does not draw hinge symbols.
