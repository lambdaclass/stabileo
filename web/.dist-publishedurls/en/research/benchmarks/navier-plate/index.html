One simply supported rectangular plate, uniform pressure, two production elements and a double Fourier series.

Model and reference

A 3 × 5 m plate, 0.1 m thick; E = 63,000 MPa, ν = 0.2; downward pressure 10 kN/m². All edges restrain vertical translation; rotations remain free (soft simple support). Three in-plane restraints remove rigid motion. The pressure is lumped as tributary nodal forces: qA/3 per triangle node, qA/4 per quadrilateral node.

At the centre, the odd sine terms alternate in sign. We sum 100 × 100 odd terms. D = Et³ / [12(1 − ν²)]. Positive displacement below means downward.

w(a/2,b/2) = 16q/(π⁶D) Σ sin(mπ/2) sin(nπ/2) / [mn(m²/a² + n²/b²)²]

m, n = 1, 3, 5, …, 199

Thin-plate series (mm): 1.283748
Published example (mm): 1.28375

Convergence

Errors are compared at equal grid subdivisions: DKT has twice as many cells, but the same nodes as MITC4. This is not a timing benchmark. DKT is a thin-plate element and converges to the thin-plate series at second order. MITC4 includes transverse shear; with only vertical translation restrained at the edges it solves a different problem with an edge boundary layer, and its centre displacement keeps rising under refinement, above both series (checked natively at 32 × 32 and 64 × 64). The thin-plate series is therefore not its reference: its smaller differences at 4×4 and 8×8 come from a change of sign, not from accuracy.

Closer to the thin-plate series (not an accuracy ranking): 4×4: MITC4; 8×8: MITC4; 16×16: DKT

Absolute-error reduction, 4×4 → 16×16: DKT against the thin-plate series: 15.78×; MITC4 with hard support against the Reissner–Mindlin series: 13.71×

Difference (%): 100 × (w / w0 − 1)

MITC4 against its own theory

With hard simple supports (the rotation along each edge is also restrained) the shear-deformable plate has its own Navier series, Reissner–Mindlin with shear correction κ = 5/6. MITC4 converges to it at second order: each refinement divides the error by about four. These three meshes are precomputed with the production engine and checked against the browser engine in tests; they are not recomputed live.

wRM = w0 + 16q/(π⁴κGt) Σ sin(mπ/2) sin(nπ/2) / [mn(m²/a² + n²/b²)]
