Topology optimization of an MBB beam

After Erik Andreassen, Anders Clausen, Mattias Schevenels, Boyan S. Lazarov, Ole Sigmund,“Efficient topology optimization in MATLAB using 88 lines of code”, Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization (2011), doi:10.1007/s00158-010-0594-7.

An educational SIMP optimizer: redistribute a fixed amount of material to reduce compliance.

The experiment

The authors’ abstract describes a compact successor to the 99-line code, adding density filtering and improving efficiency through preallocation and vectorization. Here we implement its main minimum-compliance method.

Each square uses a bilinear plane-stress element. SIMP interpolates its stiffness as E = 10⁻⁹ + ρᵖ(1 − 10⁻⁹). Optimality criteria redistribute material, with a move limit of 0.2. The engine’s sparse Cholesky solves equilibrium at every iteration.

Half-MBB beam: unit-square elements, horizontal restraints along the left edge and a vertical restraint at the lower-right corner. A unit downward load acts at the upper-left corner. E0 = 1, ν = 0.3; all quantities are nondimensional. Filter radius is measured in element widths.

Reproduction

Defaults: 60 × 20 elements, volume fraction 0.5, penalization 3, filter radius 2.4. These are section 3.4 and Figure 3(a) of the authors’ manuscript (pages 6–7). Sensitivity and density filtering use the same settings. Physical densities are shown.

Compliance is measured before each update; volume and density are recorded after it, exactly as in top88. Stop when the largest design change is at most 0.01. The reference overlay is shown only for matching default parameters.

Replay densities are rounded to 256 levels for display (maximum error 0.5/255); live densities and reported histories retain full precision.

Evidence and limitations

The reference runner downloads the authors’ unmodified function, checks its SHA-256, and observes full-precision data at its plotting calls in GNU Octave. Rust tests compare iteration counts, every compliance, volume and change, and final densities, alongside independent element and filter checks.
