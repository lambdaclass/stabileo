Where Stabileo fits

Commercial desktop packages

SAP2000, ETABS, Robot, STAAD.Pro: closed source and installed, with far more design codes and decades of use. Stabileo is open source and runs in a browser.

Open-source solvers

OpenSees, Code_Aster, CalculiX: open and powerful, run from scripts or input files. Stabileo is visual, and we compare its solver with all three.

Teaching software

Ftool, common in civil engineering courses, analyses plane frames. Stabileo adds 3D and shows the working behind each result.

01 BASIC

An open-source pocket calculator for civil engineering.

FREE, ALWAYS

Model, load, solve and read results in 2D and 3D. It shows the working, not just the answer.

  • Live calculation: results update while you drag a node or change a load.
  • The stiffness method step by step: element matrices, assembly, solution.
  • Kinematic analysis (2D) finds mechanisms, even those the counting formula misses.
  • Section analysis says which torsion theory applies: solid, Bredt or Saint-Venant.
  • P-Δ, buckling and modal in 2D and 3D; plastic collapse and influence lines in 2D.

04 STABILEO AI

An AI assistant that builds, reviews and explains models.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Ask in plain language. It works on the same model and the same solver you use: the AI proposes, the solver computes, and you decide. The design stays the engineer’s responsibility.

  • Drafts a model from a description: beams, trusses and multi-storey frames, in 2D and 3D.
  • Edits the model on screen, and shows each change before it is applied.
  • Reviews a solved model: findings by severity, the members affected, the assumptions to check.
  • Explains solver warnings and answers questions about the results.

The panel is already in the editor and works with your own Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI key, kept only in your browser; Stabileo's own AI service comes later.

05 VERIFICATION

How we check the results, and where the checks stop.

The solver runs on your computer. We compare it with seven finite-element programs and published results, and test physical invariants. Where it can, it also computes a certificate: a bound on the numerical error, its core mathematics proved in Lean for linear, modal and plastic-collapse analysis. A bound covers solving the assembled system, not whether the model is right. Defects that may have affected results in use are published as advisories.

07 PRICING

How we plan to sustain an open project

The core program, for structural analysis in the browser, is open source and free for everyone. Some specialised modules are paid; universities get those modules free. Stabileo AI and heavy cloud compute are paid by everyone, universities included, priced to cover what they cost.
