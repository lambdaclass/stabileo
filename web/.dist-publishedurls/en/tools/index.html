Free engineering calculators

Beams, columns, frames, trusses, influence lines and natural frequencies, solved with the production Stabileo engine and compared with classical formulas.

  • Simply supported beam

    Deflection, shear and bending moment of a beam on two supports.

    Open ↗

  • Cantilever beam

    Deflection, shear and bending moment of a beam fixed at one end.

    Open ↗

  • Column buckling

    Elastic critical load, first buckling mode and effective length.

    Open ↗

  • Continuous beam

    Two to four spans: support reactions, sagging and hogging moments, and deflection.

    Open ↗

  • Portal frame

    Single-bay frame with vertical beam loading and a horizontal force shared between the top corners.

    Open ↗

  • 2D truss

    Paste nodes and bars, define supports and nodal loads, and solve axial forces and displacement.

    Open ↗

  • Influence line

    Move a unit load along one or two spans; find the largest absolute response to a single axle.

    Open ↗

  • Beam natural frequencies

    First three transverse bending frequencies and engine mode shapes, compared with analytical values.

    Open ↗

Linear elastic, small displacements, prismatic Euler–Bernoulli members; truss bars carry axial force only. SI units. Self-weight excluded. Each tool states its own assumptions, sign convention and classical reference. Not a code design check.
