Closed form / Classical method

Statics for one span; force method for two spans: release the middle support and impose zero deflection there. Axle effect = axle load × influence ordinate.

Assumptions

Linear elastic, small displacements. Prismatic Euler–Bernoulli frame members; truss bars carry axial force only with ideal pin joints. Self-weight is excluded. No shear deformation, geometric nonlinearity or design checks.

Ordinates are per unit downward load. Reaction: positive upward. Moment: positive in sagging. Shear: just left of the section, positive when the part left of the section is pushed up.

References

Slope-deflection, force method, method of joints and influence lines: R. C. Hibbeler, Structural Analysis (any recent edition), chapters on those methods. Natural frequencies: R. D. Blevins, Formulas for Natural Frequency and Mode Shape (1979), Chapter 8. Portal sway: slope-deflection with axially rigid members.

Technical details

Production Stabileo solver · planar Euler–Bernoulli frame elements.

At least 32 elements per span; an influence-line section off that grid adds one node, and a section within 10⁻⁶ of the length of a grid node or of the middle support is moved onto it. Diagram extrema are sampled. Influence lines use 101 positions plus the section and its two sides; axle extrema are sampled, not an exact optimization. Shear is evaluated immediately left of the section.
