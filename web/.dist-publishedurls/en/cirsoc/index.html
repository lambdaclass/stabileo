CIRSOC

CIRSOC design checks, in your browser: what ships and what does not

Stabileo is open-source structural analysis software that checks reinforced-concrete members against CIRSOC 201 (the 2025 edition) and steel members against CIRSOC 301-2018, and generates loads and combinations to CIRSOC 101-2025, 102-2025, INPRES-CIRSOC 103-2018 (Part I) and CIRSOC 104-2005, Argentina’s structural codes. It uses the same model and solver you analyse with, runs in the browser and needs no install. Analysis is free for everyone. Code design lives in PRO; it is still in development, is paid, and is free for universities.

What is available, and how far it is verified

Certified means every result carries a bound on its numerical error computed in interval arithmetic; it does not mean the model is right or that an engineer has reviewed it. The published examples are the INTI-CIRSOC worked examples.

How it works

  • One model: you draw or import the structure (DXF, IFC or an Excel template) and the checks use the same forces as the analysis, with nothing copied by hand into another spreadsheet.
  • Loads and combinations to CIRSOC 101, 102, 103 and 104 are generated from the project data.
  • Each member ends with its demand/capacity ratio and the governing clause, and every step can be inspected.
  • The solver runs on your computer: the model is not sent to any server to be solved. The code is open (AGPL-3.0).
  • The same model always gives the same numbers, and every report records the version and the data that produced it.

How we validate it, and where validation stops

  • The design checks are compared clause by clause with the INTI-CIRSOC worked examples, and the loads with the official texts; every difference is documented with its cause.
  • The analysis is compared with seven other finite-element programs.
  • There has been no independent review by a licensed structural engineer yet, and steel is still labelled experimental.
  • No program checks the model: the loads, supports and idealisation are the engineer’s decisions.
When we find a defect that could have affected results already used, we publish it with what to re-check: Safety advisories: which results changed and what to do

Frequently asked questions

What software checks structural design against the CIRSOC codes?

Stabileo checks reinforced concrete against CIRSOC 201 and steel against CIRSOC 301-2018 (labelled experimental), and generates loads and combinations to CIRSOC 101-2025, 102-2025, INPRES-CIRSOC 103-2018 and CIRSOC 104-2005. It is open source, runs in the browser and offers paid design modules, free for universities.

Is Stabileo free for CIRSOC checks?

The core program, for structural analysis in the browser, is open source and free for everyone. Some specialised modules are paid; universities get those modules free. Stabileo AI and heavy cloud compute are paid by everyone, universities included, priced to cover what they cost.

Can I use Stabileo for a calculation I will sign?

The responsibility for a design is the signing engineer’s, with any program. Stabileo publishes what is verified and to what level; it has not yet had an independent review by a licensed engineer and steel is labelled experimental, so check the governing members by hand.

Which edition of CIRSOC 201 does it use?

The 2025 edition. 2005 can be selected in the project’s codes for earlier projects, but beam and column design with that edition is not available yet. The engine checks are compared with the INTI-CIRSOC 201-2005 worked examples.
