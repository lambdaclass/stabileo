A tied rectangular column with the same bars on the two faces across the bending direction, 10 mm ties and the clear cover given. The interaction diagram, the axial cap and the utilisation are computed by the engine’s CIRSOC 201 (2025) section kernel by strain compatibility. The squash load and the balanced point are shown beside their hand calculation. Tie spacing is the smallest of 12 bar diameters, 48 tie diameters and the least column dimension.

A teaching example, not a design check. SI units: m, kN, MPa. Published by Ergodic Group.
